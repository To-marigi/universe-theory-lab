# Final-Theory Bench v0.3 novelty-boundary research note — 2026-07-28

## Source claims

- arXiv:2603.25503v1 proves commutative collapse for two non-singular QBC
  orderings, derives constraints for CPOBC, and rejects the tested Pauli
  construction; it leaves the general noncommutative representation problem
  open.
- arXiv:2003.11311v1 gives a bounded-variation extension criterion for complex
  scalar sequential-growth measures. It is not an operator-valued CP-instrument
  extension theorem.
- arXiv:gr-qc/9904062v3 supplies the classical sequential-growth and Bell-
  causality framework; it was published as Phys. Rev. D 61, 024002.

  before evaluating interference;

  if one exists;
- generate finite CPOBC relation metadata from the transition graph instead of
  treating paper fixtures as discoveries;
- scope every representation-search failure to the exact dimension, field,
  ansatz, stratum, and relation subset actually certified;
- separate probability, instrument, and decoherence-functional extension.

## Claim boundaries

No Appendix-wide reproduction is attempted. No finite search becomes a
dimension-wide or all-finite-dimensional no-go. A diagonal decoherence
functional, if it extends, remains a classical level-1 object and is not
evidence of quantum-geometric interference. Continuum, sampler, Spin-2,
graviton, and Einstein-dynamics claims are excluded from v0.3.
