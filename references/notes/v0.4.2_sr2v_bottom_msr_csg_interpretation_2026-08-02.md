Date: 2026-08-02

## Exact project result

On the occurrence-identified transverse weak/weak chart, the observed bottom
character plus fixed-vector GC is the primitive split torus `G_m^29`. The 24
reachable-state MSR equations cut this torus down globally to a smooth,
irreducible, rational five-dimensional locus. The exact forward and inverse
maps identify it with the nonzero normalized finite-CSG parameter domain in
four couplings, times the cutoff-external `G_m(Q5)` factor.

The machine certificate is
`results/v0.4.2_sr2v_bottom_msr_global_csg.json`; the detailed derivation is
`reports/v0.4.2_sr2v_bottom_msr_global_csg.md`.

## Source / derivation boundary

The scalar CSG transition formula and the classical Bell-causality, covariance,
and Markov-sum framework are prior results. The relevant locally archived
primary sources are:

- `references/papers/gr-qc-9904062v3_rideout-sorkin_classical-sequential-growth.pdf`;
- `references/papers/gr-qc-0504066v3_varadarajan-rideout_general-classical-sequential-growth.pdf`.

They are already catalogued in `references/sources.json` and
`references/manifest.json`. The project must therefore not claim that it
discovered the finite-CSG family or the general classical classification.

What is independently derived here is narrower and computationally exact:

1. the correct weak/weak semantic placement of the 24 additive equations on
   the observed-bottom `G_m^29`, not on the separate upper `G_m^49` character;
2. their explicit 24-equation Laurent system in this finite `n<=4` ON quotient;
3. a global triangular solution, quotient-unit Jacobian, and exact inverse map;
4. identity checks against all 132 scalar coordinates, 843 operator monomial
   rows, 320 fixed-GC rows, and 24 MSR rows.

This is a reusable Paper-I lemma and search-architecture certificate. Without
a separate novelty audit it is not an independent short-report claim.

## Remaining mathematical boundary

The result classifies only the bottom scalar base. It does not solve the
separate upper `G_m^49` character, the 132-coordinate upper-right cocycle
fibre, any commutator principal open, an arbitrary rank-two state assignment,
or SR2-V. Exact fixed-state obstructions must not be promoted to a global
weak/weak obstruction unless a complete state-assignment cover is proved.

Extracted text is only a search aid. Any equation or theorem cited in a
manuscript must be checked against the archived PDFs and their page layout.
