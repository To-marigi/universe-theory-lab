## 2. Mathematical technique used this session — standard, no new citation

The gauge-weight derivation (`source_native_955_upper_stratum_v042.py`)
recovers a grading by solving a linear system over the exponent vectors of
every monomial in the streamed core. This is the standard "homogeneity space"
technique from computational commutative algebra (Cox–Little–O'Shea /
Cox–Little–Schenck lineage): the homogeneity space of an ideal is the space of
weight vectors making every generator homogeneous, computed by exactly this
kind of linear elimination. No specific paper claims priority over this
technique in a form relevant to the project; it is textbook method, not new
prior art.
