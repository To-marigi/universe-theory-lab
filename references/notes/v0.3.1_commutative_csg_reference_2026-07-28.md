## Source claims used

- Equation (15) supplies
  \(A=\lambda(\varpi,m)/\lambda(n,0)\).
- Equation (16) supplies the branch-product cylinder amplitude.
- Claim 3.8 item 2 states that a finite-coupling CSG model with one nonzero
  positive-index coupling and \(k_1>1\) has bounded variation.
- Theorem 2.1 converts bounded variation into a unique complex-measure
  extension to the sigma algebra generated by cylinder events.

The PDF layout for the spectator-independence ratio, transition formula, and
finite-coupling claim was checked on pages 8, 10, 17, and 18. Extracted text
was not treated as authoritative for equation layout.

## Project specialization and derivations

The exact specialization
\(t_0=1,\ t_2=1+i=\sqrt2e^{i\pi/4}\), with every other coupling zero, maps to
the paper's \(m=1,\ k_1=2>1\) family. The parameter was selected within that
explicit arbitrary-complex-\(t_k\), \(k\ge2\) family because it gives exact
Gaussian-rational arithmetic and a nonzero real interference term.

The identity
\(\lambda(n,0)=1+\binom n2(1+i)\) and its nonvanishing follow directly from
the specialization. This is an independent well-definedness check, not a
replacement proof for Claim 3.8.

The finite \(n=3\) interference witness between the antichain and the
two-minima/one-maximum causal set is a repository derivation from Eq. (15).
It is not a claim made explicitly in the paper.
