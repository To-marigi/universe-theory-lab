# Final-Theory Bench v0.2 research note — 2026-07-28

## Source claims

- Rideout–Sorkin supplies the classical sequential-growth template and discrete
  general-covariance/Bell-causality assumptions.
- Surya–Zalel makes infinite covariant-event extension a bounded-variation
  question for complex sequential growth.
- Srivastava–Surya proves commutative collapse for two operator orderings under
  invertibility, leaves CPOBC noncommutativity open, and rejects the tested
  Pauli construction.
- Benincasa–Dowker supplies a continuum-derived interval-action control.
- Weinberg–Witten, Weinberg soft arguments, and Deser apply only under distinct
  later-stage assumptions.

## Independent project derivations

- exhaustive unlabeled poset growth agrees with an independent relation-mask
  oracle through `n=4` and known counts through `n=5`;
- automorphism-orbit branch probabilities form exact rational instruments;
- finite diagonal decoherence functionals are strongly positive;
- selected QSG identities and Pauli residuals are checked symbolically;
- the finite phase domain is insufficient for continuum or symmetry claims.

## Unresolved gaps

The project has not reproduced every appendix parameter-elimination case in
arXiv:2603.25503v1, certified a higher-dimensional QSG representation, proven
an infinite measure extension for the Kraus candidate, or constructed a
scalable phase sampler. These gaps determine the conservative v0.2 verdict.
