# Final-Theory Program v0.1 source audit - 2026-07-28

## Source-backed boundaries

- `arXiv:2504.12919v2` evaluates a scalar retarded propagator on a supplied
  AdS1+1 causal-set sprinkling. It does not derive dimension, metric, or a
  graviton.
- `arXiv:hep-th/9206084v1` constructs a covariant closed-string quantum action
  with the BV master equation and genus-zero homotopy Lie structure. This is a
  string-consistency reference, not evidence for the causal-information model.
- `arXiv:gr-qc/0411023v3` bootstraps Einstein nonlinearities from a supplied
  linear massless spin-2 gauge field using locality and consistency. The
  emergence of that linear field remains a separate gate.
- `arXiv:1505.04753v4` relates entanglement equilibrium to the semiclassical
  Einstein equation for first-order local-vacuum variations under stated
  assumptions; nonconformal fields require an additional conjectural input.

## Independent project decisions

- Separate Spin-2 reference controls from candidate evidence in data structures.
- Treat off-shell Barnes-Rivers rank five and on-shell two helicities as distinct
  checks.
- Define the finite quantum-DAG sector exactly, but mark the variable-structure
  measure and continuum dynamics as undefined.
- Interpret `SPIN2_NOT_FOUND` as an artifact-level absence of qualifying
  evidence, not a no-go theorem for the whole model family.

## Open scientific gap

No coarse-graining map currently produces a candidate-derived symmetric
rank-two response from the quantum causal information model. Until that exists,
pole, Ward, ghost and universal-coupling tests cannot be applied to the
candidate.
