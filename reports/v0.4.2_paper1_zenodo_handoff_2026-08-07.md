## Deliberately not done

### The fresh literature gate is not cut

This is a decision, not an omission. The `2026-08-06T2315Z` predraft gate was
cut against the superseded candidate and is historical provenance only. Cutting
a replacement now would leave it stale again by the time the Sol audit and
owner approval complete, which is the exact failure mode that withdrew the
2026-08-06 candidate. The corrected manuscript wording was written to support
this separation.

Cut the gate immediately before the first draft-save, following the existing
pattern:

1. Copy `scripts/normalize_v042_paper1_zenodo_predraft_gate_20260806T2315Z.py`
   to a new timestamp and update its `SOURCE_ID`, `GATE_TRIGGER_UTC`, prior
   ledger reference, and artifact paths.
2. Run `--fetch`, then `--check`.
3. Write the dated report under `reports/` and the research note under
   `references/notes/`, and register the artifacts in `references/sources.json`
   and `references/manifest.json`.
4. **Replace the `ZENODO_PREDRAFT_*` constants in
   `verify_paper1_bundle.py`.** They currently still bind the 2315Z artifacts,
   and the bundle tests are green against that binding. Leaving them is the
   single most likely finding of the Sol audit.
5. Add the matching normalizer test under `tests/final_theory/`.
