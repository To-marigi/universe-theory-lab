## 2. Material finding that changes positioning

Yipeng Xu, [arXiv:2607.26672v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26672), submitted
2026-07-29, contains a substantial CPOBC rigidity package in Section 29.11 and
Theorem 29.12. On its self-adjoint finite-stage slice it is more general than
the project's finite cutoff, and it also treats several exact two-dimensional
triangular/extension branches. It expressly leaves non-self-adjoint
nonsingular transitions and state-only covariance among its open directions.

Consequences:

- do not claim “first `d=2` CPOBC rigidity,” “first CPOBC commutativity
  theorem,” or priority over all self-adjoint/triangular rigidity;
- describe Xu as `STRICTLY_MORE_GENERAL_ON_SELF_ADJOINT_SLICE` and
  `ADJACENT_THEOREM_OVERALL`, not as the same weak-semantics theorem;
- foreground the project's non-self-adjoint finite exact certificates and
  weak-state semantic separation;
- add a compact SR1/Xu hypothesis-reconciliation subsection before Paper I
  is drafted, so that the overlapping and incomparable domains are explicit.

The public Zenodo v0.3.9 record was created on 2026-07-31 and has publication
date 2026-07-31. Xu v1 therefore predates that public record by two days in
the overlapping territory. Internal repository chronology is not used as a
public-priority substitute.

## 5. Exact semantic boundary from the primary source

In Srivastava--Surya v1, PDF p. 15:

- Eq. (31) constructs a reachable state from a path product acting on
  `|Omega>`;
- Eq. (32) states the Markov sum on that reachable state;
- Eq. (33) then writes the corresponding operator sum as the identity;
- GC is stated as equality of the two path products acting on `|Omega>`.

This is precisely the semantic seam addressed by v0.4. The project may say
that it classifies or separates explicit formalizations of this seam within
its finite scope. It may not say that the source authors proved the weak
profile, or that the project introduced the already displayed strong
operator formulation.

The project also must not sell Eq. (120) itself as new: it is a source
relation. The independent contribution is the exact proof that six raw
source-stage CPOBC instances plus nonsingularity suffice, followed by the
source-to-direct pullback, localization, chart cover, and machine-checkable
restricted-slice theorem.

## 6. Mathematical tools adopted for v0.4

The mathematical-commutativity search changes the implementation plan in
four concrete ways.

1. **Reducible/irreducible split.** Use Florentino and generalized Shemesh
   predicates to split a common-invariant-line branch from the irreducible
   branch before heavy elimination. A common invariant line means
   simultaneous triangularizability, not commutativity.
2. **Minimal state-to-operator promotion.** For a residual space `R`, the
   exact minimal condition is injectivity of `D -> D Omega` on `R`. Cyclicity
   of `Omega` alone is insufficient and separation of the entire `M_2` is
   impossible in the defining two-dimensional representation.
3. **Sourcewise multi-probe recovery.** In `d=2`, if one MSR residual
   annihilates two linearly independent probe vectors at the same source, it
   vanishes. Ordinary single-`Omega` GC supplies only one state at each source,
   so global reachable rank two across different sources does not by itself
   recover strong MSR. The two probes require an enlarged preparation/test
   condition or another explicit sourcewise spanning hypothesis.
4. **Centralizer endpoint.** The centralizer of a non-scalar `2 by 2` matrix
   is `F[I,A]`. Proving that every `Q_i` commutes with one certified non-scalar
   `Q_k` is enough to close pairwise commutativity and may be cheaper than six
   independent commutator eliminations.

Locally linearly dependent operator results motivate low-rank/square-zero
slack searches but do not carry a CPOBC proof. Commuting-variety geometry is
a sanity check only.

[`references/notes/v0.4.2_prior_art_related_work_2026-08-01.md`](../references/notes/v0.4.2_prior_art_related_work_2026-08-01.md).

Recommended while both one-sided corners remain open:

> *Operator-strength rigidity and exact statewise noncommutativity in finite
> quantum sequential growth at dimension two*

Do not use “complete classification,” “sharp semantic threshold,” or an
unqualified “first” in the title or abstract.

### 7.2 SR3b-A title

Retain the scoped title:

> *Dimension-two commutativity on a reconstruction slice of finite CPOBC: a
> source-native Eq. (120) lemma and exact chart certificates*

The abstract should begin with the proper-slice quantifier and state in its
first paragraph that the general strong-GC/reachable-MSR profile remains
open. The main novelty sentence should name the source provenance audit,
pullback/localization theorem, and exact finite chart certificates—not claim
the source Eq. (120) as new.

1. causal-set classical and quantum sequential-growth lineage;

   (108), and (109)--(112);
