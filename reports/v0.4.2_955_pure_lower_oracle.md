# v0.4.2 955 profile: pure lower-triangular slice oracle

Status: `V042_955_PURE_LOWER_ORACLE_CERTIFIED`

This note is an independent, fail-closed verification of the checked-in
nonlinear mixed manifest under the specialization

\[
  A_e=\begin{pmatrix}p_e&0\\y_e&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

It is not a solver run, a Sage calculation, a Groebner calculation, a chart
cover, a full-profile theorem, or a counterexample certificate.

## Certified slice statement

For the 131-orbit mixed ansatz in the strong-GC + reachable-state-MSR (955)
profile, the pure lower-triangular slice satisfies all its source equations
only at \(y=0\).  The forced point is nonsingular, its four \(Q_i\) are
diagonal and commute, and it remains outside strong MSR: the source `msr:p1-0`
operator residual has constant lower-right entry one.

The check is mechanically stronger than a tangent reading in one respect:
after \(x=0\), every one of the 783 CPOBC lower-left polynomials, 320
strong-GC lower-left polynomials, and 24 reachable-MSR lower-vector
polynomials is checked directly to have degree at most one in (y).  All
diagonal/upper-right matrix entries, and the reachable upper-vector entry,
vanish identically.  Thus the selected full-rank equations are actual global
equations on this slice, not merely their first-order parts.

## Independent rank check

The tangent scout's checked-in JSON certificate supplies 131 selected labels
and QQ coefficient rows.  The oracle does not import the scout module or its
rank routine.  It re-extracts the corresponding lower-left / lower-vector
polynomials from the nonlinear manifest, specializes them at \(x=0\), compares
every coefficient exactly, and forms a new 131-by-131 `sympy.SparseMatrix`.
SymPy reports exact QQ rank 131.  Hence the selected homogeneous linear system
has only \(y=0\), which proves the slice result.

The exact artifact hashes and semantic digest are recorded in
[`results/v0.4.2_955_pure_lower_oracle.json`](../results/v0.4.2_955_pure_lower_oracle.json).

## Scope boundary

This does **not** settle solutions with \(x\ne0, y\ne0\), components
disconnected from the pure-lower or upper loci, or the unrestricted
source-native 955 system.  It does not prove full-profile commutativity and
does not certify a noncommutative witness.  Those are the remaining nonlinear
search and/or elimination tasks.
