## 2. Exact rings and cover

The 54-dimensional transverse base is a localized rational principal open, not
a pure Laurent torus. Put

```text
F_lambda = product of lambda(w,m), 0<=m<=w<=4, excluding lambda(0,0)=1,
R54 = QQ[t1,t2,t3,t4,q_b,s0,...,s48]
      [(q_b*F_lambda*product(s0,...,s48))^-1].
```

The compiler must prove that the bottom external-Q5 scalar `q_b` and the upper
Q5-only torus direction are spectators before dropping them. Only after this
gate passes may it use

```text
R52 = QQ[t1,t2,t3,t4,s0,...,s47]
      [(F_lambda*product(s0,...,s47))^-1].
```

The transition formula can have nonmonomial numerator and denominator factors
`lambda(width,maximal)` and `lambda(stage,0)`. They are units precisely because
they occur in the frozen `F_lambda` ledger. The alternative four selected
bottom coordinates `(s18,s21,s25,s26)` do not turn the whole domain into an
unlocalized four-torus: their inverse still carries the same principal-open
conditions. Encountering an irreducible denominator outside the certified
upper-monomial and lambda-factor ledger is a fail-closed manifest error, not
evidence against the Q5-free theorem.

Use denominator-safe determinant forms

```text
d_k = a1*b_k-a_k*b1,  k=2,3,4,
f_tilde = a1-b1.
```

They are declared-unit multiples of the normalized chart functions `g_k` and
`f=r1-1`. The exact cover is

```text
D(d2) union D(d3) union D(d4) union V(d2,d3,d4),
```

with `D(f_tilde)` handled on the equal-ratio remainder and `V(f_tilde)` already
safe because `delta_Q=0`.

For each non-aligned chart use `U_k=R52[d_k^-1]`. For the aligned calculation use

```text

```

## 3. Non-aligned ideals

Stream every full-`M0` Schur row through the 127-pivot elimination DAG and then
restrict it to

```text
A_i*h+B_i*w.
```

A bad obstruction has `w!=0`, so normalize `w=1` and define

```text
J_k = <A_i*h+B_i : all full-M0 Schur rows> in U_k[h].
```

The exact target is `J_k=U_k[h]`. Over a polynomial presentation, clear only
denominators factored completely over the certified upper-monomial and
`F_lambda` unit ledger, and use one Rabinowitsch equation:

```text
K_k = <cleared(A_i*h+B_i), 1-z*s_k>,
```

where `s_k` is `F_lambda` times the product of the 48 active upper torus
variables and the cleared chart factor. The ledger must prove that clearing a
base-unit denominator preserves `D(d_k)=D(dtilde_k)`. Proving `K_k=(1)` is
equivalent to the localized unit-ideal claim. The
determinantal alternative is

```text
I1(T_k)=U_k,
<A_i> subset sqrt(I2(T_k)),
```

for `T_k=[A B]`.

## 4. Aligned ideals


The primary computational route covers nonzero `(w2,w3,w4)` by the three

## 7. Proper-ideal and witness handling

A proper ideal over `QQ` guarantees only an algebraic-closure point. It is not
an exact rational witness. If an exact `QQ` point is extracted:

1. verify every torus coordinate and the appropriate chart factor;
2. invert every certified monomial coordinate change;
3. reconstruct `Q1,...,Q4` with Eq. (120), setting the free Q5 direction to zero;
4. reconstruct the 127 non-`Q` coordinates by exact pivot back-substitution;
5. directly evaluate all 1,127 `M0` rows, determinant predicates, and a nonzero
   commutator;
6. test each relevant branch-specific Eq. (113)/Eq. (139) addition separately,
   never combining literal and derived Eq. (113).

A point killed by all supplemental branch rows is only a common-core escape,
not a project-level weak-semantics witness.
