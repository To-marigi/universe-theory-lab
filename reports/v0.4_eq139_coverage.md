## Strong-GC redundancy and finite coverage

Write `A=A_n^(m)`, `B=A_n^(k)`, `Q=Q_n`, and `R=Q_(n+1)`. Eq. (139) is

`A B R B^-1 Q^-1 B = B A R A^-1 Q^-1 A`.

Equivalently, if
`X=A B R B^-1 Q^-1` and `Y=B A R A^-1 Q^-1`, it is the strong-GC path
identity `X B=Y A` obtained by the source's Eq. (84)--(91)-style Bell and
conjugation substitutions. Hence an instance is semantically redundant
when its corresponding operator-GC square is assumed.

and `n=3` squares have endpoint stages 4 and 5 and lie within that stage
horizon. The `n=4` square would have endpoint stage 6 and is not covered.
This is the precise materialisation gap inherited by v0.3.9.
