## Certified conditional statements

Let `R` be the declared linear space of admissible residual operators and let
`V` be the probes applied to the same residual. The evaluation map is

`ev_V: R -> (QQ^2)^V, D |-> (Dv)_(v in V)`.

Statewise equality promotes every `D in R` to the operator identity exactly
when this map is injective. This is the minimal condition for the specified
residual family; a vector need not separate all of `M_2(QQ)` when `R` is
smaller.

The machine certificate verifies:

- one probe on full `M_2` has evaluation rank 2 and kernel dimension 2;
- the basis probes `e_1,e_2` give evaluation rank 4 and recover all of `M_2`;
- one probe `e_1` already separates the smaller space `span{I,E_21}`;
- if the same `2 by 2` residual kills two independent probes, symbolic
  determinant elimination forces all four residual entries to zero;
- cyclicity does not imply separation: `e_1` is cyclic for the defining
  `M_2` representation, while nonzero `E_12` kills it;
- the centralizer of every non-scalar `2 by 2` matrix `A` is `QQ[I,A]`.

For the centralizer theorem, the exact adjoint matrix has rank-two minors
`b^2`, `c^2`, and `-(a-d)^2` in the three non-scalar branches. Since `I` and
`A` are independent kernel elements, its kernel is exactly their span.

## Essential nonclaim: global reachability is not sourcewise recovery

The exact counterexample

`v_1=e_1, D_1=E_12` and `v_2=e_2, D_2=E_21`

has `span{v_1,v_2}=QQ^2`, while `D_1 v_1=D_2 v_2=0` and both residuals are
nonzero. Thus states belonging to different source residuals cannot be pooled
to promote either residual.

In the ordinary single-`Omega` CPOBC semantics, GC gives one cylinder state at
each source. The two-vector theorem is therefore a
multi-preparation/multi-probe additional assumption applied to the same source
residual, not a consequence of global reachable rank two.

## CPOBC binding

- fixed-vector GC tests `D_(alpha,beta)=P_alpha-P_beta` on `Omega`;
- reachable-state MSR tests `D_c=sum_j A_(c,j)-I` on the matched state
  `v_c=P_alpha Omega`;
- if evaluation is injective for every GC and MSR residual family, both
  semantics promote to the strong operator profile;
- only then may the frozen nonsingular `d=2`, `n<=4`, ON v0.3.9 theorem be
  invoked to force `Q_1,...,Q_4` to commute.

The artifact does not assert that the base profile supplies the additional
probes. It closes neither one-sided profile and does not decide the SR2-V
visible-witness search.
