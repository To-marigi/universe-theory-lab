# v0.4.2 721 Gröbner preflight: corrected residual-unit measurement

Status: **preflight only — no solver run against the 721 profile**

## Why this correction exists

The first preflight recorded in `e989dff` selected individual scalar entries
from 2×2 residual matrices while labeling them as residuals.  Its reported
`1 → 2 → 3 residuals` progression was therefore misnamed.  Its selection
score was also a coefficient L1 weight rather than the reported polynomial
term count.  That measurement is superseded and is not used as a quantitative
claim about the full system.

The corrected worker selects complete CPOBC residual units.  A unit contains
one whole 2×2 residual and all of its nonzero scalar entries are passed to
`sympy.groebner()` together.  Units are selected by total scalar term count,
then maximum entry size, degree, and stable IDs.  The cache is separate from
the predecessor scalar-entry cache.

## Method and safeguards

- source: reduced CPOBC residuals after the already-completed inverse-token
  substitution and single-token defining-relation reduction;
- selection: smallest complete residual units, not individual matrix entries;
- one residual unit contributes up to four scalar polynomial equations;
- any of the five variables `(D, a, b, c, d)` of an inverse-token relation
  triggers inclusion of that full relation;
- each Gröbner subprocess has a hard 90-second timeout;
- cache construction has a separate 180-second timeout;
- no full 721 residual inventory is sent to a solver.

## Corrected result

| complete residual units | scalar equations | relation equations | variables | solver time | basis |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4 | 2 | 14 | 1.078s | 30 |
| 2 | 8 | not completed | not completed | **timeout at 90s** | — |

The driver stopped after the second unit timed out; a third-unit run was not
attempted.  Building the 200-unit selection cache took 129.354s and is
separate preprocessing, not Gröbner time.

The first selected unit was
`cpobc-relation-01e29996483e2c4f342c/eq103`; it had 76 total scalar terms,
four nonzero matrix entries, maximum entry size 20, and degree 5.

## Interpretation

This is a materially stronger warning about rapid growth in the current
SymPy tooling than the predecessor entry-wise measurement, but it is still a
best-case CPOBC probe, not a representative run of all 1,967 residuals.  It
does **not** prove that every Gröbner implementation or dedicated CAS will
fail, and it does not justify a numerical extrapolation to full-system
completion time.

What is established independently is the stronger bounded-algebra result:
the inverse closed-form substitution and the confluent single-token defining
relation reduction were applied exhaustively to all 1,967 residuals and
closed zero new residuals.

No unbounded Gröbner computation was run.  The remaining owner choices are a
dedicated CAS feasibility assessment or merging 721 into the same unresolved
resource-limit decision as 955.

The two scratch JSON files used for cache/driver communication were removed
after verification; the measured result is recorded in
`CURRENT_RESEARCH_STATE.json` and this report.
