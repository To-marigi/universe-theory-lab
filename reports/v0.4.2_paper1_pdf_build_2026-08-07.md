stating the relation between the C2 witness and the `d=2` obstruction recorded
by the source. The obstruction concerns self-adjoint Pauli representations
under the full operator CPOBC conditions; the C2 witness is non-self-adjoint,
upper triangular, and established only under the weaker statewise conditions.
The paragraph states that the witness therefore does not contradict that
result and must not be read as a Pauli-type or full-operator-CPOBC
representation.

digest was changed. The page count remains 18.
