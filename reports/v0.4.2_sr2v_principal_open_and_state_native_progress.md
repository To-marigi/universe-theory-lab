## 1. Nonlinear upgrade of the aligned obstruction

Let `y` be the 131 lower-left coordinates of the actual transition orbits and
let `z` denote all remaining localized coordinates.  The earlier tangent
certificate selected 131 lower residuals whose Jacobian in `y` is nonsingular
at the frozen aligned witness.  Each selected residual vanishes identically
when `y=0`.  Therefore, in the determinant-localized coordinate ring, the
selected system has an exact polynomial factorization

```text
F(y,z) = H(y,z) y.
```

The artifact records the exact nonzero base value

```text

= 5517109901165625 /
  63657374260452690195888927762793067532858387302060507832379389042324415617604272068231168.
```

Consequently `Delta_align=det H` is not the zero polynomial.  On the nonempty
principal open `Delta_align != 0`, every solution of the selected residuals,
and hence every solution of the full system, has `y=0`.  All reachable states
there remain on the aligned invariant line and every upper-triangular
commutator annihilates that line.

This is a nonlinear algebraic obstruction, not only a tangent-space result.
Its boundary is equally important: it says nothing on `Delta_align=0`, on
disconnected components, on the transverse reducible chart, or on irreducible
representations.

## 2. Generic splitting of a transverse two-character family

Consider the transverse upper-triangular family with `Omega=e_2`, whose two
diagonal characters are transitive-percolation characters with rational
parameters `s` and `r` in the nonsingular scalar domain
`r*s*(1+r)*(1+s) != 0`.  The upper-right coordinates form a homogeneous
linear system with 1,187 rows and 131 in-cutoff variables.  The scalar family
was checked symbolically against all of the following ledgers:

- 783 raw CPOBC equations;
- both 25-record Eq. (113) branches, kept separate;
- four printed-strict and ten completed Eq. (139) instances, audited as
  separate domains (the strict subset is not duplicated in the row matrix);
- all 1,529 GC path pairs;
- all 24 MSR polynomial identities.

For `r != s`, simultaneous triangular conjugation always supplies the
coboundary kernel

```text
x_e = c (b_e(r) - a_e(s)).
```

At the exact sample `(r,s)=(1,2)`, a stored `130 by 130` minor is nonzero, the
actual rank is 130, and the nullity is one.  Its reduced numerator defines a
nonzero polynomial `Delta(r,s)`.  Thus on the nonempty principal open
`r != s` and `Delta != 0`, the kernel consists only of the coboundary, every
in-scope extension of the 131 actual transitions splits by one global
conjugation, and `Q_1,...,Q_4` commute.  On the same-character line `r=s`, the
same in-scope commutativity holds directly.  Supplemental `Q_5` is assigned its
diagonal scalar-character value, while its upper-right extension coordinate is
fixed to zero and excluded from the 131-variable splitting kernel.

The exceptional off-diagonal locus `Delta=0`, general scalar characters, and
other reducible components remain open.

## 3. Complete commutator-pivot branch cover

If `Q_1,...,Q_4` are noncommutative, at least one of their six pair
commutators is nonzero.  Fix such a pair and write

```text
C_ij = [Q_i,Q_j],
tau_ij,e = tr(C_ij A_e),
d_ij = det(C_ij Omega, Omega).
```

Exact `2 by 2` identities give the following exhaustive four-way split on
each nonzero pivot:

1. `det C_ij != 0`: the pair is irreducible;
2. `det C_ij = 0` and some `tau_ij,e != 0`: a triple is irreducible;
3. all `tau_ij,e=0` and `d_ij != 0`: globally reducible, transverse initial
   vector;
4. all `tau_ij,e=0` and `d_ij=0`: globally reducible, aligned initial vector.

In the transverse reducible branch, the rational basis

```text
S_ij = [C_ij Omega, Omega]
```

normalizes `Omega=e_2` and `C_ij=E_12` with no residual conjugation gauge.
The transition generators are upper triangular and can be written

```text
A_e = b_e [[r_e,z_e],[0,1]].
```

The pivot noncommutativity equation is

```text
b_i b_j ((r_i-1)z_j - (r_j-1)z_i) = 1.
```

In particular, `det[Q_i,Q_j]` is identically zero inside this reducible chart
and must not be used as its noncommutativity saturation.  The supplemental
`Q_5`, which has no `n<=4` transition occurrence, remains a separately tracked
operator when Eq. (113) and Eq. (139) are solved.

## 8. Revised exact search order

The remaining search is now organized by explicit algebraic boundaries:

1. use the primitive `G_m^49` and `G_m^29` Laurent maps instead of finite
   scalar-torus resampling, and impose the 24 additive bottom MSR equations;
2. bind the approved solver budget and a separate `det Q_5` localization to
   the compiled D12 manifest, then determine whether its relation variety
   meets `D12!=0`; only after this pair patch treat structured `beta` systems;
3. target the aligned degeneracy locus `Delta_align=0`, since the complementary
   open is closed exactly;
4. target the exceptional transverse locus `Delta(r,s)=0`, then enlarge from
   transitive-percolation pairs to the exact scalar-character locus;
5. use the six commutator pivots to separate transverse reducible,
   pair-irreducible, and triple-irreducible branches;
6. directly certify any rational hit against all relation, inverse,
   determinant, rank, and visibility ledgers.

Generic resampling inside either certified principal open has low value.  A
finite-field or floating-point point remains a scout until reconstructed over
`QQ` and checked by the independent direct oracle.
