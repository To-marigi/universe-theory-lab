## 3. Complete commutator-pivot branch cover

If `Q_1,...,Q_4` are noncommutative, at least one of their six pair
commutators is nonzero.  Fix such a pair and write

```text

tau_ij,e = tr(C_ij A_e),
d_ij = det(C_ij Omega, Omega).
```

Exact `2 by 2` identities give the following exhaustive four-way split on
each nonzero pivot:

1. `det C_ij != 0`: the pair is irreducible;
2. `det C_ij = 0` and some `tau_ij,e != 0`: a triple is irreducible;
3. all `tau_ij,e=0` and `d_ij != 0`: globally reducible, transverse initial
   vector;
4. all `tau_ij,e=0` and `d_ij=0`: globally reducible, aligned initial vector.

In the transverse reducible branch, the rational basis



```text

```

In particular, `det[Q_i,Q_j]` is identically zero inside this reducible chart
and must not be used as its noncommutativity saturation.  The supplemental
`Q_5`, which has no `n<=4` transition occurrence, remains a separately tracked
operator when Eq. (113) and Eq. (139) are solved.
