# v0.4.2 721 CPOBC/MSR re-expanded under saturated inverse tokens

Status: **`V042_721_CPOBC_MSR_RESATURATED_NO_NEWLY_ZERO_RESIDUAL_OPEN`**

[`source_native_721_inverse_saturation_v042.py`](v0.4.2_721_inverse_saturation.md)
gave the 8 inverse tokens exact algebraic content: `A^-1 = (1/det A) *
adj(A)` via one `D_<token> = 1/det(A)` variable per token. This gate
re-expands the same 783 CPOBC and 24 strong-MSR word equations that
[the scalar gate](v0.4.2_721_scalar_gate.md) compiled, with every
inverse-token matrix replaced by that closed-form definition, and asks:
does saturating the inverse tokens close any previously-open (scalar
nonzero) residual?

## Result

**No.** Zero of the 700 previously scalar-nonzero CPOBC residuals and zero
of the 21 previously scalar-nonzero strong-MSR residuals become zero under
saturation.

| inventory | equations | newly zero | still nonzero |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CPOBC | 783 | 0 | 700 |
| strong MSR | 24 | 0 | 21 |

MSR's saturated scalar-term statistics (7,704 total terms, maximum 186) are
**identical** to the unsaturated scalar gate's. **Correction (verified
after the fact, see the successor
[inverse-token incidence gate](v0.4.2_721_inverse_token_incidence.md)):**
this is *not* because MSR words are free of inverse tokens — 21 of 24 do
contain one (e.g. `Q_1^-1`, `Q_2^-1`). The term count is preserved because
the substitution `M_<token>_INV_ij -> D_<token> * M_<base>_kl` maps one
single-variable monomial to one single-variable-pair monomial: it is
monomial-to-monomial, not monomial-to-sum, so it cannot by itself change
how many *distinct* monomials a polynomial has unless two different
monomials collide after substitution. For MSR that collision never
happened; for CPOBC's total (3,013,576 terms,
maximum 48,007 in one equation at degree 21) differs from the unsaturated
scalar gate's total (3,557,570 terms, maximum 58,604 at degree 15): both the
term total and the single largest equation went *down*, not up. Introducing
`D_<token>` does add a variable and pulls in the full adjugate of the base
matrix wherever an inverse token appears, but for the equations that
actually contain one, this evidently produces more internal (partial, not
full) cancellation than it adds — a real but non-obvious net effect, not
the "more variables means more terms" assumption one might expect going in.

## Safeguards

The gate fails closed in the other direction too: if saturation had made
any previously-*zero* residual nonzero, that would indicate a bug in the
inverse definition (since a word identity is zero regardless of how any
particular token's matrix is represented), and the gate raises rather than
reporting a false result. This did not occur (`unexpectedly_nonzero_count`
== 0 for every residual, checked).

## What remains

The 1,529-pair fixed-vector GC inventory is not resaturated by this gate.
No elimination combining the 8 `D_<token>*det-1=0` defining relations with
the CPOBC/MSR/GC residuals themselves has been attempted — this gate only
checked direct substitution, not solving the combined system.

No full 721 commutativity theorem, witness, or obstruction is issued.
