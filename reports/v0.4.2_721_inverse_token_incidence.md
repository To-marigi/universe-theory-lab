# v0.4.2 721 inverse-token incidence: a structural ceiling, not a small one

Status: **`V042_721_INVERSE_TOKEN_INCIDENCE_COUNTED_STRUCTURAL_ELIMINATION_CEILING_OPEN`**

Before attempting any heavier elimination combining the 8 `D_<token> *
det(A) - 1 = 0` defining relations with the residual system, this gate asks
a purely structural question at the free-word level, with no scalar
expansion: of the 1,967 residuals that direct substitution left nonzero
(700 CPOBC + 21 strong-MSR + 1,246 GC), how many even *contain* an inverse
token in the first place? A residual that never mentions one cannot be
touched by any manipulation of the inverse-token relations, substitution or
elimination alike — there is nothing for those relations to act on. This
bounds what an elimination could possibly close, for free, before running
one.

## Result

| inventory | nonzero | contains inverse token | does not |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CPOBC | 700 | 700 | 0 |
| strong MSR | 21 | 21 | 0 |
| GC | 1,246 | 1,240 | 6 |
| **total** | **1,967** | **1,961** | **6** |

**1,961 of 1,967 (99.7%) contain an inverse token.** This is not the cheap,
decisive ceiling a smaller count would have given — it does not let 721
close on structural grounds alone the way 955's `minor_cover_preflight`
did. Only 6 GC pairs are structurally out of reach of any inverse-relation
manipulation; essentially the entire nonzero inventory remains a live
target for elimination.

## A correction to the predecessor report

Computing this exposed an error in
[the CPOBC/MSR resaturation report](v0.4.2_721_inverse_resaturated_cpobc_msr.md):
it claimed strong-MSR's unchanged saturated term count (7,704, identical to
unsaturated) was because "no MSR constraint's word actually contains an
inverse token." That was never checked — it was inferred from the numeric
coincidence and was wrong. **21 of 24 MSR words do contain an inverse
token** (e.g. `Q_1^-1`, `Q_2^-1`), confirmed directly here.

The term-count equality has a different, correct explanation: the
substitution `M_<token>_INV_ij -> D_<token> * M_<base>_kl` replaces one
single-variable monomial with one product-of-two-variables monomial. That
map is monomial-to-monomial, not monomial-to-sum, so it cannot change how
many *distinct* monomials a polynomial has unless two different monomials
collide into the same one after substitution. For every MSR equation, none
did. The predecessor report and its test have been corrected in place to
state this rather than the false "no inverse tokens present" claim.

## What this does and does not mean for 721

This does not mean elimination will succeed — it means elimination cannot
be dismissed as pointless on cheap structural grounds, the way it could
have been if the count had come back small. If an elimination is
attempted, it has to engage with essentially the whole system, not a
convenient corner of it.

No full 721 commutativity theorem, witness, or obstruction is issued.
