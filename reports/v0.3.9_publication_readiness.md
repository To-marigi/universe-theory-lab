## Remaining blockers

1. **The permanent-publication decision is human-owned.** A Zenodo DOI cannot be
   withdrawn, so the owner must decide that this tree is what should be bound to
   one and that `.zenodo.json` is correct. No agent performs the deposit.
2. **JOSS scope and maturity review** — for JOSS only, and not a gate on a
   Zenodo deposit.

## A correction to the v0.3.8 assessment

The v0.3.8 readiness artifact listed a single
`SOFTWARE_MATURITY_REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW` item covering release history,
support expectations *and* JOSS scope, and presented it as gating publication in
general. That overstated the barrier to a Zenodo deposit.

Zenodo applies no editorial review. For a deposit the questions are only whether
this content should be permanent, whether the metadata is right, and whether
this is the version to publish first. JOSS fit is a separate gate that applies
when submitting to JOSS, and can be assessed afterwards. The two are listed
separately here.
