## Artifact role

The generated PDF is an untracked local observation.  Its raw SHA-256 above
records this dated run, but it is not a required reproducibility-contract hash
and the ordinary manuscript validator must pass in a fresh clone where the PDF
is absent.  The tracked helper, reproduction protocol, report, pinned image,
and source bindings define how to repeat the layout/build check.  A future PDF
with a different raw hash is not automatically a scientific failure; its log,
source bindings, page layout, and reason for the byte-level difference must be
reviewed and recorded.
