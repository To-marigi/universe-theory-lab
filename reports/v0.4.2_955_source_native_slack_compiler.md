Status: **`V042_955_SOURCE_NATIVE_SLACK_INVENTORY_READY_NO_SOLVER_RUN`**

This artifact is the first source-native implementation boundary for the
strong-GC plus reachable-state-MSR (`955`) repair.  It is a provenance and
coverage verifier, not a Gröbner campaign and not a commutativity proof.

## What is compiled

The compiler keeps all 165 raw transition occurrences and verifies their ON
identification into 131 quotient orbits (34 alias occurrences).  For each of
the 24 raw source MSR rows it identifies the unique timid occurrence and
records the source recurrence

```text
T_c = I - sum(non-timid A) + u_c (J v_c)^T,
J = [[0,-1],[1,0]].
```

The 24 two-coordinate vectors `u_c` contribute exactly 48 scalar slack
coordinates.  Since `(J v_c)^T v_c=0`, all 24 reachable-state-MSR matrix
residuals become 48 identically-zero vector-entry constraints.  The `u=0`
slice recovers the **raw source strong-operator-MSR** equations only; it does
not claim to reconstruct the frozen Eq. (108)-expanded Q presentation.

Strong GC is retained through its 320 source-path spanning-basis word
equalities.  The compiler independently rebuilds the endpoint graph from 407
enumerated paths: its 87 endpoint fibres are all connected trees, so the
recomputed unordered same-endpoint-pair total is 1,529.  The evidence and its
digest are emitted in the result JSON; this is not a copied headline count.
The raw source system retains all 783 CPOBC word equations.  In `d=2`, the declared
base scalar namespace is 524 quotient-operator entries plus 48 slack
coordinates, hence 572.  The 783 CPOBC, 320 strong-GC, and 24 timid-definition
matrix blocks account for 4,508 scalar entry equations before localisation.

therefore exactly covered by 48 coordinate principal-open patches

started.

## Supplemental gates remain separate

Eq. (113) has 25 derived-`Q_n` and 25 literal-`Q_(n+1)` records.  They remain
separate because the source indexing is ambiguous.  Their stored path records
depend on Eq. (112) B definitions, so this weak-MSR compiler treats both as
**validation-only, fail-closed** downstream gates rather than importing them
as source coordinate equations.

Eq. (139) likewise remains two domains: four printed-strict instances and ten
Eq. (145)-completed instances.  Under strong GC an instance is derived only
when its corresponding GC square is within the compiled endpoint scope.  The
present inventory covers the `n=2` and `n=3` square stages but not the `n=4`
stage-six endpoint.  Therefore all 4/10 instances remain direct
witness-validation gates for a full `n<=4` result; neither domain is silently
merged or claimed as current source-coordinate coverage.

Any future witness must directly substitute all 25/25 Eq. (113) records and
all 4/10 Eq. (139) records, preserving those branches/domains.

## What this artifact is not

The result is a structured **word/matrix-block inventory**.  It is not yet an
expanded scalar-polynomial solver manifest, has no completed localisation or
chart-cover proof, and makes no global 955-profile coverage claim.
