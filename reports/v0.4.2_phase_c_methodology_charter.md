Phase C turns the repository's verification discipline into an explicit research
artifact. It does not reopen the frozen 955 or 721 computation, and it does not
add a new scientific verdict to either profile.

## Four required controls

### 1. Semantic identity

Every machine-readable evidence artifact has a canonical semantic digest. Digest
verification must operate on the canonical object with its digest field removed;
line-ending changes must not change the semantic identity. A raw-byte hash, when
needed for release preservation, is recorded separately and never substituted for
the semantic digest.

### 2. Claim-boundary ledger

Each report separates three layers:

1. source claims imported from papers, specifications, or prior project records;
2. exact project derivations and machine-checked measurements;
3. unresolved gaps, resource limits, and owner decisions.

A measurement such as “no witness found in a bounded search” cannot be promoted to
an obstruction theorem without a separate finite proof certificate.

### 3. Verdict grammar

Verdict strings must state both scope and epistemic status. In particular,
`OPEN_RESOURCE_LIMIT` means that the declared computation stopped at a resource
boundary. It must not be read as `EMPTY`, `OBSTRUCTED`, `COMMUTATIVE`, or
`NONCOMMUTATIVE`.

### 4. Frozen artifacts and budget separation

Once a result is used as evidence, later interpretation is recorded in a new
artifact rather than by silently rewriting the predecessor. Preflight measurements
are distinct from an authorized solver campaign. A solver campaign requires a
versioned toolchain, input digest, resource budget, timeout policy, and owner
authorization before execution.

## Initial Phase-C inventory

The initial frozen inputs are:

- the Phase-A termination decision packet;
- the Phase-A freeze execution record;
- the 955 DM/block-determinant evidence;
- the corrected 721 inverse-relation and Gröbner-preflight evidence;
- `MISSION.md` and `CURRENT_RESEARCH_STATE.json` as scope and state authorities.

The first implementation gate is a machine-readable claim-boundary ledger and
frozen-artifact index. It must include predecessor paths, semantic digests,
scope, claim boundary, and whether a result is terminal, nonterminal, or an owner
decision record.

## Reopening policy

Reopening 955 or 721 is outside the active Phase-C work. It requires a new owner
decision plus a versioned budget artifact. For 721, a dedicated CAS toolchain must
first reproduce the corrected small residual-unit benchmark. For 955, any renewed
matching search must provide a finite theorem route or be stopped by the same
resource-limit rule.

## Phase-B boundary

Phase B remains deferred until Phase C is complete. Its target is the required
physics derivation audit, not a deeper computation of the frozen finite
955/721 profiles.

## Acceptance criteria for this charter

- all Phase-A frozen inputs are indexed without changing their bytes;
- semantic digests are independently rechecked;
- claim boundaries are machine-readable and tested;
- preflight and authorized-budget records are separate;
- the global state remains `FINAL_THEORY_OPEN` and does not claim a complete
  finite ON semantics lattice.
