# Control-and-methods scoped paper outline

Working title:

> A fail-closed reproducibility framework for finite quantum sequential growth:
> exact controls, independent replay, and scaling limits

## Scope statement

This manuscript reports the auditable control and methodology layer of the
project. It does not claim that the active candidate model is physically valid,
that a continuum limit has been established, or that the finite ON semantics
lattice is complete.

## Proposed manuscript

1. **Introduction** — why candidate/control separation, replay, and explicit
   resource boundaries are necessary.
2. **Semantic and authorization model** — claim-boundary ledger, verdict grammar,
   frozen-artifact policy, and budget separation.
3. **Exact bounded controls** — finite sampler fixtures, fixed-ticket replay,
   relabeling checks, and the four-dimensional Minkowski positive control.
4. **Independent replay and runtime safety** — primary/independent digests,
   ticket allocation regression, runtime guards, hard-supervisor fixtures,
   atomic promotion, and fail-closed handling.
5. **Scaling boundary** — the exact random-growth control reaches the n=38
   down-set cap boundary; primary and independent routes agree; no approximate
   fallback is used.
6. **Candidate boundary** — the frozen candidate weight is nonlocal, no exact
   reduced-state factorisation is certified, and candidate access is therefore
   excluded from the reported evidence.
7. **What remains unresolved** — production spectral/statistical freeze, BDG
   ensemble, N=60 control, full 955/721 profiles, and final theory claims.
8. **Reproducibility package** — versioned JSON artifacts, semantic digests,
   Docker/Sage environment, test commands, and immutable Paper I provenance.
9. **Conclusion** — the method is auditable and reproducible within its bounded
   scope; physical conclusions remain open.
