## 3. Global Schur identity

Order the columns as the 127 non-`Q` coordinates, the relevant internal `Q`
coordinates, and external `Q5`.  The pivot block and an arbitrary M0 row have
the form

```text
pivot: [P U 0]
row:   [v w 0].
```

The predecessor certificate proves `det(P)` is a Laurent unit on the entire
declared 54-dimensional transverse base.  Hence

```text
[v w 0] - v P^-1 [P U 0] = [0, w-v P^-1 U, 0].
```

The last entry is identically zero.  No new localization is introduced.  This
is a dimension-free block identity; a generic symbolic 2 by 2 proxy is also
stored as an implementation guard.

## 6. Exact next-gate contracts

For a non-aligned chart, the ring `R` below means the Eq. (120) chart ring
`S_k=R_base[g_k^-1]`.  Thus, when a computation is performed in the original
base ring, every displayed unit-ideal equality is read after saturation by
`g_k`.  For the aligned calculation, `R` means
`(R_base/(g2,g3,g4))[f^-1]`, where `f=r1-1`; equivalently one first adds the
equal-ratio ideal and then saturates by `f`.

For a non-aligned Schur matrix `T=[A B]`, a kernel vector with normalized
obstruction coordinate `w=1` exists exactly when all equations
`A_i h+B_i=0` have a common solution.  Pointwise safety is therefore:

- on every `D(A_j)`, `rank(T)=2`;
- on `V(A_1,...,A_m)`, `rank(B)=1`.

Equivalent certificate routes are

```text
(I2(T) : A_j^infinity) = R  for every j,
<A_1,...,A_m,B_1,...,B_m> = R,
```

or globally

```text
I1(T)=R and <A_1,...,A_m> is contained in sqrt(I2(T)),
```

or the auxiliary ideal

```text
<A_i h+B_i : 1<=i<=m> = R[h].
```

Over the original base, clear chart denominators to obtain
`J_tilde` in `R_base[h]` and certify
`(J_tilde:g_k^infinity)=R_base[h]`.

For the equal-ratio aligned Schur bookkeeping
`T=[A B2 B3 B4]`, write `B=[B2 B3 B4]`.  Pointwise safety is:

- on every `D(A_j)`, `rank(T)=4`;
- on `V(A_1,...,A_m)`, `rank(B)=3`.

Equivalent routes are



## Claim boundary

This proves only Q5-freeness of full `M0` and of its global Schur reduction on
the transverse base.  It proves neither the non-aligned `(A,B)` nor aligned
`(A,B2,B3,B4)` rank conditions, no row-module membership, no witness, and no
SR2-V terminal.  Eq. (113) and Eq. (139) require their branch-specific audit if
common-core commutativity fails.  Finally, the equal-ratio **aligned
remainder** used here is bookkeeping inside the transverse problem; it is not
the separate structural `ALIGNED_GLOBALLY_REDUCIBLE` branch.
