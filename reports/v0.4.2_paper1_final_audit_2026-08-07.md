# Paper I v0.4.2 pre-deposit audit — 2026-08-07

## Scope and a stated limitation

This audit covers the 2026-08-07 rebuild and rebinding of the Paper I v0.4.2
deposit candidate, the manifest/validator chain, the Zenodo bundle, and the
deposit documents.

**Limitation, stated up front:** most of the changes audited here were made in
the same session that produced this audit. A self-audit is weaker than an
independent one, particularly for judgement calls about wording and scope. The
mechanical results below were re-derived from disk by a script that assumes
nothing written earlier is correct, but the editorial findings deserve a second
reader.

## Method

1. Every hash in every document was recomputed from the file on disk and
   compared against the manifest, the validator constants, the build report,
   and the handoff record.
2. The manuscript was checked for the withdrawn recency wording, and every
   bound required fragment was resolved against the live source.
3. The visual-QA evidence directories were inspected directly: render count
   and pixel dimensions, and every page changed by the rebuild was read.
4. The Zenodo bundle was actually built, in both production and preview mode,
   and strict verification was run against the result.
5. A repository-wide search looked for surviving references to the superseded
   candidate, with the immutable dated records excluded from the search.

### F3 — RESOLVED. The manuscript now addresses the source's own d=2 negative result

Srivastava–Surya (arXiv:2603.25503), the manuscript's direct source, states in
its abstract that a Pauli-matrix representation of the antichain subalgebra
generators leads to inconsistencies, implying that a non-trivial representation
must be higher dimensional. The manuscript constructs an exact rational
noncommutative witness at `d=2`.

There is no contradiction: the source's negative result concerns self-adjoint
Pauli generators under the full operator CPOBC conditions, while C2 is a
non-self-adjoint upper-triangular assignment in `GL_2(Q)` satisfying only the
statewise conditions. The project's own prior-art audit records this as "the
Pauli appendix is not arbitrary `GL_2`".

At the time this finding was raised, the string `Pauli` did not appear anywhere
in `main.tex`: the manuscript devoted a section to bounding its claims against
Xu but never addressed the closer source's `d=2` statement, so a reader
comparing the source's abstract with C2 would suspect a conflict before finding
the resolution.

**Resolved.** The owner directed the addition, and a paragraph was added to the
adjacent-results section stating the scope of the obstruction, the reasons the
C2 witness does not contradict it, and that the witness must not be read as a
Pauli-type or full-operator-CPOBC representation. The manuscript was rebuilt and
all reflowed pages were re-inspected. The candidate hash in this audit's table
is the rebuilt one.

### F4 — REVIEW. No HAL bundle exists

Deposit is planned for Zenodo and HAL. Only `zenodo/paper1-v0.4.2/` exists.
HAL requires its own document-type, domain-classification, and licence
declarations, and its own file layout. Nothing HAL-side has been produced: no
metadata worksheet, no checklist, no verifier. The Zenodo bundle is not
transferable as-is.

### F5 — INFORMATIONAL. PDF byte reproducibility is bounded by the toolchain

Two independent rebuilds from the pinned image differ from the candidate, and
from each other, in exactly 66 bytes confined to `/CreationDate`, `/ModDate`,
and `/ID`. All content and cross-reference bytes are identical. The manifest
already declares the PDF hash a dated observation rather than a reproducibility
contract, and this is now empirically substantiated.

Making the hash contractual would require a deterministic build
(`SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` with `FORCE_SOURCE_DATE`). That would change the candidate
bytes and invalidate the completed visual QA, so it was not done. It is a
reasonable improvement for a future version, not for this one.

## Disposition

The manuscript, manifest, validator, test, and document chain is internally
consistent and verified. The candidate PDF is a faithful product of the
recorded source.

F1 and F3 are resolved. The deposit still cannot proceed: F2 remains blocking,
and its resolution is defined, namely to cut the fresh gate immediately before
the first draft-save and rebind `ZENODO_PREDRAFT_*`. F4 is an unstarted work
item.

One further owner action is now outstanding and is recorded in the handoff: the
current PDF bytes were produced and QA'd by the assistant session after the
owner directed the F3 addition, so an owner acceptance of these exact bytes
must be recorded before any draft-save. Both the builder and the verifier bind
the sentence stating this, so its removal fails closed.

Nothing in this audit authorizes draft creation, DOI reservation, manuscript
freeze, deposit, publication, or a push.
