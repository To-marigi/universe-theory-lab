# v0.4.2 721 bounded scalar gate

Status: **`V042_721_SCALAR_CPOBC_MSR_COMPILED_GC_STAGED_OPEN`**

The source-native free-word IR was expanded into generic 2-by-2 scalar
polynomials for the finite CPOBC and strong-MSR inventories.  This is a
bounded compilation gate, not a commutativity proof: matrix inverse equations,
determinant saturation, elimination, and the fixed-vector GC system remain
outside its scope.

## Exact result

| inventory | equations | word identities | scalar zero matrices | scalar terms | maximum entry |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CPOBC | 783 | 83 | 83 | 3,557,570 | 58,604 |
| strong MSR | 24 | 3 | 3 | 7,704 | 186 |

The CPOBC expansion has 2,800 nonzero scalar matrix entries and maximum
monomial degree 15.  Strong MSR has 84 nonzero entries and maximum degree 5.
Every word identity maps to the zero generic matrix, and no nonidentity word
residual maps to zero in this gate.  Per-equation digests and size statistics
are stored; the multi-million-term polynomial bodies are deliberately not
stored.

The observed wall-clock time for the first run was about 98.7 seconds on the
workspace runtime.  This timing is a measurement, not a semantic claim.

## Calculation safeguards

- The input source-native IR is recompiled and its Eq. (107)/(108) frozen word
  digests are checked before scalar expansion.
- Free-group cancellation is therefore applied before matrix expansion.
- Matrix products preserve word order; only scalar monomials are canonicalised
  as commutative products.
- Inverse tokens remain independent scalar variables.  No inverse relation or
  nonzero-determinant saturation is silently imposed.
- The checked-in JSON has a self-consistent semantic SHA-256 digest and uses
  LF-only line endings.
