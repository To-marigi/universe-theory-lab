## Outcome

`LITERATURE_FRONTIER_OPEN`

The standard fixed-2-by-2 algebra, simultaneous-similarity tools,
triangularisation criteria, trace invariants, and general noncommutative
rational-realization theory are prior art.  None of the audited sources
supplies the project-specific finite `n<=4` CPOBC rational reconstruction,
its reverse-equivalence proof, branch-separated Eq. (113) systems, or a
complete exact S1/S2/S3 classification.  Accordingly,
`EXTERNAL_RESULT_SUPERSEDES_TASK = false`.

## Target-paper status on 2026-07-28

The [official arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25503) still listed
only v1, submitted 2026-03-26, with no journal reference or journal DOI.
The [INSPIRE record](https://inspirehep.net/literature/3136377) had no
publication or DOI entry and reported zero citations at the audit snapshot.
An exact-title Crossref query returned no exact work.  Exact arXiv-ID/title,
author-record, CPOBC/operator-GC terminology, correction/erratum, and
citation-route searches found no source that corrects Eq. (113) or resolves
the general d=2 CPOBC representation problem.

This is a reproducible, bounded absence finding.  It is not a claim of
worldwide nonexistence.

## Classification of the v0.3.4 work

### `LITERATURE_LOCKED`

- Cayley--Hamilton/adjugate reduction of an invertible fixed 2 by 2 matrix.
- General simultaneous similarity and triangularisation criteria.
- Trace, determinant, and word-trace invariants, subject to their field and
  genericity hypotheses.
- General noncommutative rational matrix-evaluation and realization theory.
- Target-paper Eqs. (103)--(114), Lemma 3.10, and its restricted Pauli test.
- Classical atomisation definitions already archived in the repository.

### `REGRESSION_ONLY`

- Checking the implementation's 2 by 2 inverse against the adjugate formula.
- Similarity deduplication through known invariants/canonical forms.
- Checking localization and saturation plumbing against standard algorithms.
- Reproducing the target paper's Pauli-proportional inconsistency.

These checks may validate implementation, but cannot be announced as new
theorems.

### `OPEN_TARGET`

- Rationally reconstructing the v0.3.3 finite CPOBC DAG at fixed `d=2`.
- Proving both directions between full operator assignments and the
  reconstructed rational scheme.
- Constructing separate finite systems for both Eq. (113) source branches.
- Exhausting all S1/S2/S3 charts for `n<=4` with exact denominator and
  saturation predicates.
- Certifying an exact finite noncommutative representation, only
  commutative finite solutions, or finite inconsistency at the declared
  scope.

### `FORMULATION_MISMATCH`

General free-skew-field/minimal-realization theorems do not automatically
become a fixed-`d=2` commutative-coordinate reconstruction theorem.  The
matrix-centred realization literature also does not identify the
project-specific `B` operators or prove CPOBC reverse equivalence.

### `SOURCE_AMBIGUITY`

- Eq. (113): derived/appendix `Q_n` versus literal printed `Q_(n+1)`.
- Page 19: the isolated non-invertibility sentence versus the surrounding
  nonsingular/inverse-based profile.
- Operator GC/MSR: reachable-state statements must not be silently promoted
  to strong operator identities.  v0.3.4 explicitly audits
  `PAPER_STRONG_OPERATOR_PROFILE` and keeps reachable-state semantics
  separate.

## Claim ceiling

Even a complete exact `n<=4`, `d=2` result would be finite algebraic
evidence.  It would not establish an infinite QSG representation, vector
measure, continuum limit, emergent spacetime, spin two, or graviton.  The
literature audit therefore licenses only the bounded v0.3.4 algebraic task
and the continuing overall verdict `FINAL_THEORY_OPEN`.
