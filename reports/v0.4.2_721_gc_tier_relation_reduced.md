## What this settles and what it does not

This settles the question the [bounded probe](v0.4.2_721_inverse_relation_reduction_probe.md)
was built to answer honestly: the reduction was cheap (measured, not
assumed) and it was run to completion over the entire inventory, not
declared infeasible without trying. The result is a genuine, checked
negative: this specific reduction method, applied exhaustively, does not
close 721.

It does **not** mean no elimination whatsoever could ever close 721 — only
this one (a confluent reduction using each defining relation once per
occurrence pattern, with no cross-relation S-polynomial computation, since
none was needed given disjoint variables). A full Groebner basis
computation over the combined ideal (all 1,967 residuals plus the 8
relations) is a categorically different and far heavier undertaking that
has not been attempted and is not casually feasible with this project's
plain-Python exact-arithmetic tooling.

No full 721 commutativity theorem, witness, or obstruction is issued.
