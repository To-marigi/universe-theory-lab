## Combined with CPOBC/strong-MSR

Across the whole 721 native IR, saturating the 8 inverse tokens closes
**zero** of the 1,967 previously scalar-nonzero residuals (700 CPOBC + 21
strong-MSR + 1,246 GC). Every category — word/scalar-zero agreement, no
previously-zero residual turning nonzero, no previously-nonzero residual
turning zero — was checked exactly, not assumed; every tier's compile
function fails closed (raises) if any of these invariants breaks.

## What this does and does not mean

This is a real, checked negative result about *direct substitution*: giving
the inverse tokens their correct algebraic content does not, by itself,
force any additional cancellation anywhere in the compiled CPOBC, strong-MSR,
or GC inventories. It does **not** mean the inverse relations are useless —
only substitution was tried here. No elimination combining the 8
`D_<token>*det(A)-1=0` defining relations *with* the 1,967 nonzero residuals
themselves (e.g. treating them as a joint polynomial system and asking what
follows) has been attempted. That is a fundamentally different and heavier
kind of computation than direct substitution, and is not started by this
gate.

No full 721 commutativity theorem, witness, or obstruction is issued.

## Safeguards and boundary

Each tier gate independently re-derives the C(k,2) star-pair structural
check used by the unsaturated tier gates, cross-checks the input inverse
saturation gate's own digest, and fails closed on either direction of
unexpected zero/nonzero status change.
