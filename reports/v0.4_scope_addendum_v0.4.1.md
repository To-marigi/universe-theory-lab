# v0.4 occurrence-semantics scope addendum

Date: 2026-08-01

This addendum narrows one scope statement in the pre-existing
`results/v0.4_weak_d2_classification.json` without changing that
content-addressed research artifact.

## Certified claim

The exact rational v0.4 construction is a certified noncommutative witness for
the finite, nonsingular, `d=2`, `n<=4`, fixed-vector-GC plus
reachable-state-MSR profile with occurrence identification
`ON_QUOTIENT`.  All 165 records checked by that artifact are
reduction/alias-catalog assignments over 131 frozen quotient orbits.  They are
not 165 independent transition occurrences.

## Claim not yet certified

The naturally labelled occurrence-identification-OFF domain has 50 source
nodes and 406 transition occurrences through source stage four.  A direct OFF
certificate requires labelled lifts of CPOBC, MSR, Eq. (113), and Eq. (139),
followed by exact substitution into all resulting residuals.  Those lifts do
not yet exist in the repository.

Consequently, the machine artifact's statement that the ON assignment is
automatically an OFF witness must be read only as an orbit-constant transfer
candidate.  It is not a proof for `OFF_NATURALLY_LABELLED`, and no OFF theorem
is currently claimed.

## Provenance policy

The v0.4 JSON is retained byte-for-byte because later inventories and tests
bind it by hash.  This addendum, the v0.4.1 handoff, and the publication
roadmap are the authoritative scope correction for subsequent research and
writing.  The witness arithmetic itself is unaffected.
