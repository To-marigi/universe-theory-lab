## 1. Purpose

Before depositing SR3b-A or assembling Paper I, perform a version-specific
audit of related papers, prior results, analogous finite-dimensional
classification work, and relevant technical reports. The audit must decide
what is genuinely supplied by the project, what is already present in the
source literature, what is only methodologically adjacent, and what remains a
bounded negative search result.

This audit does not change the certified mathematical scope of SR3b-A. It
controls only novelty, priority, citation, positioning, title, abstract, and
publication decisions.

## 2. Mandatory search tracks

1. **Direct CPOBC/QBC source lineage**
   - arXiv:2603.25503, every available version, its bibliography, citing
     papers, author follow-ups, conference notes, and technical reports;
   - explicit occurrences of CPOBC, quantum Bell causality, operator Bell
     causality, and the source Eqs. (103)--(120).
2. **Causal-set sequential-growth lineage**
   - classical sequential growth, complex/quantum sequential growth,
     covariant growth, quantum measure, and finite-stage operator
     formulations;
   - papers that impose sum rules, path-independence/covariance, or causality
     only on a state, cyclic vector, or reachable subspace.
3. **Closest theorem-level analogues**
   - finite-dimensional representation and rigidity results;
   - matrix commutativity criteria, commutator identities and commutator
     ideals, commuting varieties, simultaneous triangularisation and
     simultaneous diagonalisation, common invariant-subspace criteria,
     Burnside-type irreducibility criteria, and classifications specific to
     two-by-two matrix families;
   - cyclic/separating-vector, locally linearly dependent operator, and
     statewise-to-operator promotion theorems that may partially replace a
     strong GC or strong MSR assumption;
   - exact noncommutative witnesses under weakened statewise semantics.
4. **Computational and proof-certificate analogues**
   - exact rational witnesses, Gröbner/saturation certificates, chart covers,
     source-to-presentation elimination, and provenance-aware computer algebra;
   - method papers are not counted as prior instances of the CPOBC theorem
     unless they state an equivalent mathematical result.
5. **Short reports and project-overlap audit**
   - the public Zenodo v0.3.9 record and every project short report;
   - arXiv, Zenodo, HAL, INSPIRE, Crossref, journal sites, and author pages for
     overlapping preprints or reports;
   - distinguish bibliographic mirrors/index records from independent works.

## 3. Required evidence fields

For every materially relevant source, record:

- authors, title, venue/repository, identifier, exact version and date;
- canonical URL, retrieval date, and legally accessible local filename;
- exact pages/equations/theorems used;
- relationship to this project: `DIRECT_SOURCE`, `SAME_CLAIM`, `STRICTLY_MORE_GENERAL`,
  `STRICTLY_WEAKER`, `ADJACENT_THEOREM`, `METHOD_ONLY`, or `BACKGROUND`;
- which project claim it supports, limits, duplicates, or leaves untouched;
- an explicit claim boundary separating source statements, project
  derivations, and unresolved interpretation.

Search-engine snippets and citation counts are discovery aids only. A novelty
decision requires inspection of the underlying paper or authoritative record.
A bounded search with no match is not proof that no prior result exists.

## 4. Repository preservation workflow

Before new web searches, inspect `references/sources.json`,
`references/manifest.json`, `references/text/`, and `references/notes/`.
For every new material source:

1. preserve a version-specific lawful copy under `references/papers/` when
   practical;
2. append bibliography, exact URL, retrieval date, local filename, intended
   use, and claim boundary to `references/sources.json`;
3. run `scripts/archive_references.py` to validate the file, update SHA-256,
   refresh `references/manifest.json`, and create searchable text;
4. add a concise interpretation note under `references/notes/` whenever
   scientific judgment affects novelty or manuscript positioning;
5. never overwrite an earlier version and verify equations/page layout in the
   PDF rather than relying only on extracted text.

After local validation, mirror every paper file used or newly acquired during
this audit to the NAS directory
`Y:\universe-theory-lab-backup\references\papers\`, preserving filenames and
versions. Verify the copied file by SHA-256 against the local copy. The NAS is
a backup, not the authoritative working copy; do not delete or overwrite a
different-version NAS file merely because a newer version exists locally.

- duplicate/overlap risks for a standalone SR3b-A preprint and Paper I;
- recommended wording for title, abstract, introduction, and nonclaims;
- unresolved searches, unavailable sources, and the search cutoff date.

Allowed terminal states are:

- `PRIOR_ART_AUDIT_COMPLETE_DISTINCT_CONTRIBUTION_IDENTIFIED`;
- `PRIOR_ART_AUDIT_COMPLETE_OVERLAP_REQUIRES_RESCOPING`;
- `PRIOR_ART_AUDIT_OPEN_SOURCE_OR_COVERAGE_LIMIT`.

Until one of these states is supported, retain
`NOVELTY_PRIORITY_AUDIT_PENDING`, do not use “first”, “novel”, “complete
classification”, or “sharp threshold”, and do not authorize deposit or journal
submission.
