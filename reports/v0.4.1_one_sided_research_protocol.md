## 4. `ON_QUOTIENT` relation-list ablations and failed soundness gate

The v0.3.7 `Q_5`-free shared core contains 976 matrix relations:

| family | count |
|---|---:|
| CPOBC | 700 |

Two exact relation-ID ablations were frozen before the solver run:

| target profile | conservative proof core | relation count |
|---|---|---:|
| `FIXED_GC_STRONG_MSR` | CPOBC + strong MSR | 721 |
| `STRONG_GC_REACHABLE_MSR` | CPOBC + strong GC | 955 |

They were intended as sufficient no-go cores, but the required one-way logic
fails. The ambient Q coordinates were compiled under
`PAPER_STRONG_OPERATOR_PROFILE` before relation selection:

- the 955 relation list retains Eq. (108) reconstruction derived from strong
  MSR, although reachable-state MSR was intended;
- the 721 relation list retains Eq. (112) reconstruction derived from strong
  GC, although fixed-vector GC was intended.

The exact ideals are therefore restricted slices, not conservative supersets
of the target profiles. Their commutativity cannot be lifted to those
profiles. A profile-native compiler must ablate assumptions in both relation
records and coordinate definitions.

The existing 21 `R_2,R_3,R_4` S1/S2 charts may be reused for these ON cores only
because retained CPOBC Eq. (120), together with `det(Q_1)!=0`, implies pairwise
commutation of `R_n=Q_1^-1 Q_n`. The chart cover is not reusable for OFF until
the labelled reduction and its corresponding Eq. (120) premises are compiled.

The weak side must be represented as vector equations, not deleted from the

A witness may not omit the weak equations.

## 6. Eq. (113) and Eq. (139)

The following inventories are never merged:

- Eq. (113), derived `Q_n` branch: 25 relations;
- Eq. (113), literal `Q_(n+1)` branch: 25 relations;
- Eq. (139), printed-strict domain: 4 instances;
- Eq. (139), Eq. (145)-completed domain: 10 instances.

Within the frozen strong profile, the v0.3.7 `Q_5`-free core excludes the Eq.
(112)/(113) path family. This is safe for that strong-profile forward no-go
proof but is not a one-sided result or a redundancy theorem. Eq. (139) is
redundant when the corresponding strong-GC square is available, while its
`n=4` endpoint lies outside the frozen local-GC endpoint coverage. In a
GC-weakened profile it must therefore remain a separately declared gate rather
than being imported by implication.

For OFF, both equation families need occurrence-local lifts. A representative
chosen only by precursor cardinality is not an occurrence-wise equation.

## 7. Execution order and proof gates

1. Freeze the relation IDs, source hashes, semantic-profile names, and the
   ON/OFF namespace in a static v0.4.1 inventory artifact.
2. Run exact counterexample scouts first. `NO_WITNESS_IN_SCOUT` is not a
   nonexistence result.
3. Only if no certified witness is found, run the two ON sufficient-core
   `QQ` chart campaigns.
4. A finite field is a scout only. Only proof-eligible `QQ` certificates may
   close a chart.
5. A profile is proved commutative only when every compulsory chart, every
   localisation factor, and every target commutator component is covered.
6. A candidate becomes a counterexample only after direct exact substitution
   into the full profile and both separately retained equation inventories.
7. Any timeout, missing backend, incomplete chart cover, or failed binding
   leaves the profile open with a resource-limit record.
