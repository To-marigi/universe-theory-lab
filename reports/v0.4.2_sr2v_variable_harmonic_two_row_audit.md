## Exact harmonic parameter space and gauge

stages 1--4 and 63 terminal endpoints at stage 5. The reachable-state MSR

```text

```

Restriction to the 63 terminal boundary values is therefore a linear

```text

```

Root evaluation is the nonzero linear functional

```text

```

where all 63 integral path-multiplicity weights `w_T` are reconstructed from
the ledger. Hence `ker(ev_root)` has dimension 62. A frozen normalized CSG

```text

```

The nonzero condition is exactly rank two because the root state is `(1,0)`.
After the residual global scaling `k -> b k`, this fixed-`h` family is
`P^61`.

For comparison, allowing both harmonic coordinates gives root-normalized
ordered pairs `h(root)=1, k(root)=0` of dimension `62+62=124`. The global
basis gauge preserving `Omega=e1` is


Its rank-two quotient is the relevant open of `Gr(2,63)`, of dimension 122.
Thus the CSG-primary fixed-`h` family is only a 61-dimensional subfamily and
