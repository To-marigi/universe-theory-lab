# v0.4.2 721 CPOBC/MSR reduced modulo the defining relations: still no closure

Status: **`V042_721_CPOBC_MSR_REDUCED_MODULO_DEFINING_RELATIONS_NO_NEWLY_ZERO_OPEN`**

The [bounded probe](v0.4.2_721_inverse_relation_reduction_probe.md) measured
a fast, correct reduction (mean 3.5ms, max 15.5ms per residual on a
24-residual sample; the single worst known CPOBC equation, 186,116
saturated terms, reduced in 2.8s). This gate runs that same exact
reduction — rewriting `D*a*d -> 1+D*b*c` per token, binomial-expanded,
confluent because the 8 tokens' relation variables are pairwise disjoint —
over the full 783 CPOBC and 24 strong-MSR word equations.

## Result

**No newly-zero residual.** Zero of the 700 previously scalar-nonzero
CPOBC residuals and zero of the 21 previously scalar-nonzero strong-MSR
residuals close under reduction, exactly as substitution alone left them.

| inventory | equations | newly zero | still nonzero |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CPOBC | 783 | 0 | 700 |
| strong MSR | 24 | 0 | 21 |

The reduction is genuine algebraic work, not a no-op relative to the
resaturation gate's plain-substitution output:

- **CPOBC** total term count *shrinks further*: 3,013,576 (saturated) ->
  2,304,462 (reduced). Maximum single-equation term count drops from
  48,007 to 32,099; maximum monomial degree drops from 21 to 18 — using the
  relations, not just substituting them, removes real structure.
- **strong MSR** total term count *grows slightly*: 7,704 (saturated,
  identical to unsaturated) -> 7,880 (reduced). The binomial expansion of
  repeated `D*a*d` occurrences can add terms as well as remove them; here
  it added a few.

Neither direction produced a zero.

## Safeguards

As with every gate in this chain, the compile function fails closed in
both directions: it raises if any previously-zero residual becomes nonzero
under reduction (a correctness bug, since a word identity's matrix is zero
regardless of the token representation used), and it separately checks
zero-status agreement between word-level and saturated-level results
before reduction even runs. Neither triggered.

## What remains

The 1,529-pair fixed-vector GC inventory is not reduced by this gate — a
[separate tiered sweep](v0.4.2_721_gc_tier_relation_reduced.md) runs the
identical method over it. Together, CPOBC + strong-MSR + GC will give the
full answer to whether the defining relations, actually used (not merely
substituted), close any of the 1,967 originally scalar-nonzero residuals.

No full 721 commutativity theorem, witness, or obstruction is issued.
