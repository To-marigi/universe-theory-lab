## 1. Executive finding

The 42 exact QQ chart calculations and their independent certificate oracle
are algebraically correct for the systems they actually received.  They do
**not**, however, prove either intended one-sided semantic profile.

The frozen direct/Q presentation has
`semantic_profile = PAPER_STRONG_OPERATOR_PROFILE`.  Before relation-family
selection, it uses Eq. (108), which depends on strong MSR, to reconstruct
timid and non-timid transitions, and Eq. (112), which depends on strong GC, to
reconstruct non-antichain gregarious generators.  Removing explicit MSR or GC
relation records therefore does not remove the corresponding strong axiom
from the coordinate domain.

The correct current status is:

| profile, occurrence identification ON | status |
|---|---|
| strong GC + strong MSR | commutativity proved by v0.3.9; unaffected |
| fixed-vector GC + strong MSR | **open**; v0.4.1 used hidden Eq. (112)/strong-GC consequences |
| strong GC + reachable-state MSR | **open**; v0.4.1 used hidden Eq. (108)/strong-MSR consequences |
| fixed-vector GC + reachable-state MSR | exact rational noncommutative witness from v0.4; unaffected |

Thus `WEAK_D2_ON_ONE_SIDED_COMMUTATIVITY_PROVED` and the claimed sharp
“at least one strong side” threshold must be withdrawn as semantic theorems.
The truth or falsity of both one-sided statements remains unresolved.

## 5. Repair alternatives

### A. Profile-native source-coordinate repair — recommended

Compile the ON quotient directly with 131 transition-orbit matrices and 165
aliases.  Retain all 783 CPOBC equations, all determinant gates, and only the
GC/MSR semantics actually selected by the profile.  Do not use Eq. (108) in
the weak-MSR profile or Eq. (112) in the fixed-vector-GC profile.

Proceed counterexample-first, then exact elimination only if no rational
witness appears.  This is the safest route and produces a reusable compiler
for the later 406-occurrence OFF track.  Its cost is materially higher than
the 132-variable Q presentation; Gröbner feasibility under 8 GiB is unknown.

### B. Two smaller profile-specific repairs

- For fixed-vector GC + strong MSR, Eq. (107)/(108) may be retained, but keep
  the 24 gregarious `G_c` operators independent and do not apply Eq. (112).
- For strong GC + reachable-state MSR, retain Eq. (112) only through a
  derivation that does not reconstruct its `B` factors via Eq. (108); keep
  timid operators or sourcewise slack operators explicit and impose
  `N_c v_c = 0` rather than `N_c = 0`.

This may be computationally smaller, but two compilers create more proof and
equivalence obligations.

### C. Publish an asymmetric interim result

Re-scope the short report to the sound statements: strong/strong
commutativity, weak/weak noncommutative witness, and the exact bounded
one-sided scouts.  State both general one-sided corners as open.  This is the
fastest publication path, but it loses the sharp semantic-threshold theorem.

## 6. Recommendation and requested decision

Adopt A as the repair track and C as the immediate writing posture: correct
the knowledge base now, keep the current certificates as restricted-locus
data, and run a source-native counterexample-first campaign before making any
new one-sided theorem claim.  Defer relation-level minimality until the
profile-native compiler exists.

No further Sage/Singular campaign for the affected profiles should run until
the new compiler proves that its parameter space is a superset of the target
source semantics.  The already approved standard budget remains suitable for
scouting and per-chart attempts, but feasibility must be reported honestly as
`OPEN_RESOURCE_LIMIT` if the source-coordinate system exceeds it.

## 7. Owner resolution and refined implementation order

The owner independently verified the Eq. (108) dependency, the 24/117/24
transition census, the exact escape point, and the 35-test regression, then
approved repair. The implementation order is refined as follows:

1. remove withdrawn forward-rule/verdict paths from the live driver and
   oracle without rewriting historical result JSON;
2. verify whether the existing 955 certificates prove the partial theorem on
   `P intersect image(Phi)`, including all localisation predicates;
3. extend only the missing reachable-MSR directions with
   `N_c=u_c(Jv_c)^T`, two parameters for each of 24 sources, and prioritise
   `N != 0` counterexamples;
4. use a full 131-orbit compiler as the semantic coverage verifier, not the
   default Gröbner coordinate system;
5. treat the fixed-vector-GC side separately by restoring the 20
   Eq. (112)-eliminated non-antichain generators and seek an explicit escape
   point.
