## Visibility domain

The primary domain is `ALL_COMPILED_CYLINDER_STATES_IN_COMMON_H`: each tested
vector is reconstructed from a genuine path and retains its endpoint causet,
stage, and relation data. Two secondary diagnostics use only antichain cylinder
states or states in a prefix before both commutator indices.

This is an operator-action diagnostic in the common Hilbert-space
representation. It is not a claim that the mixed-stage word `Q_i Q_j` is
itself a legal chronological growth path, and it is not called a direct
physical measurement protocol.
