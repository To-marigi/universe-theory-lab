# Finite phase diagram

The frozen sparse-Kraus candidate and random-growth negative control are
evaluated at `n=3,4,5` without coefficient retuning.

Recorded observables include order fraction, interval-abundance vector, mean
height and width, longest-chain and maximal-antichain ratios, automorphism
entropy, order-fraction susceptibility, a Binder-like cumulant, and
distribution entropy.

Myrheim–Meyer dimension, midpoint-scaling dimension, curvature distribution,
correlation length, and scaling exponents are `null` by design. The exact
domain is too small to define them defensibly.

No first-order, continuous, or crossover classification can be separated from
finite-size artifacts. The phase classification is
`FINITE_SIZE_ARTIFACT_UNRESOLVED`.
