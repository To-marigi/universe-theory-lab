# v0.3 high-dimensional CPOBC representation search

Verdict: `CPOBC_SEARCH_INCONCLUSIVE`.

No exact noncommutative representation was found, and no no-go theorem was
proved.

## Exact bounded search

The new work starts at \(d=3\) and evaluates 14 deterministic exact matrix
points in dimensions three and four over:

- \(\mathbb Q\);
- Gaussian rationals \(\mathbb Q(i)\);
- \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\);
- \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\).

The point library includes commuting pipeline controls and representatives of
affine matrix-unit/rank-one perturbations, reducible Pauli block lifts,
Heisenberg–Weyl clock/shift matrices, quadratic-field affine matrices and
companion-matrix words.

Each point records:

- exact determinants, traces and invertibility;
- all pairwise commutators;
- joint centralizer dimension;
- generated algebra span;
- coordinate invariant subspaces and a conservative reducibility status;
- characteristic polynomials and Jordan-stratum diagnostics;
- exact residual matrices and hashes for necessary Eqs. 119, 130 and 163;
- all intermediate invertibility conditions for the representative
  \(S_1/S_2\) construction.

The commuting controls pass. No tested noncommuting point survives all necessary
relations. The scoped conclusion for each rejected point is only
`NO_CANDIDATE_IN_DECLARED_EXACT_GRID`.

## Why this remains inconclusive

The search does not exhaust continuous parameters, similarity strata or all
requested ansatz families. Dense symbolic, separate weighted-shift and
permutation-plus-diagonal systems, and a complete reducible-but-indecomposable
classification were not run. There is no full assignment of the 641 compiled
transition relations and no dense Gröbner/component decomposition.

Accordingly, the following are not issued:

- `CPOBC_NONCOMMUTATIVE_REPRESENTATION_FOUND`;
- `CPOBC_NO_REPRESENTATION_UNDER_ASSUMPTIONS`;
- a dimension-wide or ansatz-class-wide no-go.

The exact residuals are useful rejection certificates for the 14 listed points,
not for the surrounding parameter families.
