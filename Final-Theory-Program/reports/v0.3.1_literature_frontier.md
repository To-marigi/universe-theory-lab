## Follow-up, correction, and generalization chains

- [1007.2725](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.2725) poses the vector-measure
  extension problem; [2003.11311](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.11311) gives a
  later criterion in scalar Complex Sequential Growth. This is a specialization,
  not an operator-valued generalization.
- [hep-th/0302111](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0302111) refines the original
  [Quantum Causal Histories](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9904009) formulation
  with event algebras and CP maps.
- [quant-ph/0104027](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0104027) proves the general
  semicausal-to-semilocalizable conjecture left open for arbitrary operations in
  [quant-ph/0102043](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0102043).
- [2008.02607](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02607) is a structural follow-up to
  [1910.07292](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.07292); both remain classical.
- [Cyclic quantum causal models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.12157) generalizes
  the acyclic framework of [1906.10726](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.10726), but
  still does not address CPOBC sequential growth.
- The official [1512.02240 version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.02240)
  states that v2 weakened the causality definition and v3 made minor
  publication-stage edits.
- No correction, refutation, higher-dimensional construction, or general no-go
  follow-up to [2603.25503](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25503) was found.

used to verify equations or figures. Semantic Scholar's HTTP 429 and
OpenAlex's lack of an indexed target record are also explicit unresolved
limitations.

## Gate decision

The research fork is the open branch:

```text
known extendible scalar Complex Sequential Growth positive control
→ complete finite CPOBC relation compilation
→ exact d=3 noncommutative representation or assumption-scoped no-go
```

The Kraus/CP-channel spectator problem remains a separate
`FORMULATION_MISMATCH` track. Existing histories, records, QCH, semicausal,
comb, causal-box, and covtree results receive no novelty credit.
