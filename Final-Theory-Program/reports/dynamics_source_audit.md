# Dynamics primary-source audit

All archived PDFs, hashes, and extracted text are recorded in
`references/manifest.json`.

## Rideout–Sorkin, arXiv:gr-qc/9904062v3

- Definition used: natural labeling, down-set precursor growth, discrete
  general covariance, and Bell-causality spectator comparison.
- Source result: a classical stochastic sequential-growth family follows under
  the paper's assumptions.
- Boundary: classical transition probabilities are not noncommuting quantum
  transition operators.

## Surya–Zalel, arXiv:2003.11311v1

- Definition used: covariant events live beyond the finite cylinder event
  algebra.
- Source result: bounded variation is necessary and sufficient for the stated
  complex-measure extension; positive-real couplings provide an extending
  control family.
- Boundary: finite-stage normalization does not imply extension of the
  operator or Kraus candidate to all covariant events.

## Srivastava–Surya, arXiv:2603.25503v1

- Definition used: TOBC, NTOBC, CPOBC, transition-operator algebra, and
  antichain subalgebra.
- Source results: TOBC and NTOBC give commutative algebras under the stated
  invertibility assumptions; CPOBC leaves noncommutativity open; the tested
  Pauli construction is inconsistent.
- Open part: a general higher-dimensional noncommutative representation is not
  constructed or ruled out.

## Benincasa–Dowker, arXiv:1001.2725v4

- Definition used: interval-abundance curvature/action control.
- Source result: under continuum-approximation hypotheses, the nonlocal causal
  set operator approaches the continuum d'Alembertian plus curvature term.
- Boundary: continuum approximation is an assumption of the control. It cannot
  prove that the candidate dynamics generates a continuum phase.

## Weinberg–Witten, DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(80)90212-9

The theorem is tracked by its separate Lorentz-covariant current/stress-tensor
hypotheses. It is not summarized as a blanket prohibition on background-
independent emergent gravity.

## Weinberg, DOI 10.1103/PhysRev.135.B1049

The S-matrix, asymptotic-state, pole, and supplied massless-spin assumptions are
registered as later gates. They are not inputs to v0.2 dynamics.

## Deser, arXiv:gr-qc/0411023v3

The self-coupling construction starts from a massless spin-two gauge field. It
is a post-Spin-2 gate and is not used to generate the microscopic action.
