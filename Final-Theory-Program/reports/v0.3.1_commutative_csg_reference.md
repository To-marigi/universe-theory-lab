## Source and exact parameter mapping

The implementation follows Surya and Zalel,
*A Criterion for Covariance in Complex Sequential Growth Models*,
arXiv:2003.11311v1 (2020-03-25), archived as
`references/papers/2003.11311v1_surya-zalel_covariance-complex-sequential-growth.pdf`
with SHA-256
`0db0461c144e36215eccfc5b51c52720da4575878876f18c4a796041a639f272`.
The equation layout was checked in the PDF, especially pages 8, 10, 17, and
18; extracted text was used only as a search aid.

The paper defines

\[
\lambda(a,b)=\sum_{k=b}^{a}\binom{a-b}{k-b}t_k,\qquad
A(C_n\rightarrow C_{n+1})=\frac{\lambda(\varpi,m)}{\lambda(n,0)}
\tag{15}
\]

and the cylinder amplitude as the product of transition amplitudes along its
branch (Eq. 16). The selected exact specialization is

\[
t_0=1,\qquad t_2=1+i=\sqrt 2e^{i\pi/4},\qquad
t_k=0\quad(k\ne0,2).
\]

This is not an invented family: in the notation of Section 3.3.1 it has one
nonzero positive-index coupling, so \(m=1\), \(k_1=2>1\), \(s=\sqrt2\), and
\(\phi=\pi/4\). Claim 3.8 item 2 therefore gives bounded variation. Theorem
2.1 then gives the unique extension from the cylinder event algebra
\(\mathcal Z\) to its generated sigma algebra \(\mathcal S_{\mathcal Z}\);
the paper's covariant quotient events are consequently measurable in this
scalar CSG scope.

As an independent well-definedness check,

\[
\lambda(n,0)=1+\binom n2(1+i)
\]

never vanishes: its real part is \(1+\binom n2>0\). This denominator argument
is a project derivation. It is not the bounded-variation or infinite-extension
proof.

The scalar Bell audit uses the zero-safe product form of the paper's Eq. (7),

\[
A_{\rm full}(P_1)A_{\rm red}(P_2)
=A_{\rm full}(P_2)A_{\rm red}(P_1),
\]

so zero transition amplitudes do not produce invalid ratios. The ratio form
also agrees on all 327 pairs for which its denominators are nonzero.

Path multiplication and a second, direct endpoint formula were checked
against one another. For a finite order \(C\), the direct formula multiplies
the \(\lambda(|{\rm past}(x)|,m_x)\) numerator over elements \(x\), divided by
\(\prod_{j=0}^{|C|-1}\lambda(j,0)\). This supplies an independent,
manifestly relabel-invariant oracle rather than rerunning the path code.

## Exact physical interference witness

At \(n=3\), take the mutually exclusive covariant singleton events

- \(A=E[p3\text{-}000]\): the three-element antichain, canonical rows
  \([0,0,0]\);
- \(B=E[p3\text{-}024]\): two incomparable elements below one maximal
  element, canonical rows \([4,4,0]\).

These are physically different unlabeled causal-set geometries. Each event
has exactly one distinct natural birth labeling, so the witness does not rely
on label multiplicity or two natural labelings of the same causal set.

\[
\operatorname{Amp}(A)=\frac{2-i}{5},\qquad
\operatorname{Amp}(B)=\frac{3+i}{5}.
\]

With
\(D(A,B)=\overline{\operatorname{Amp}(A)}\operatorname{Amp}(B)\),

\[
D(A,B)=\frac{1+i}{5}\ne0,\qquad
D(B,A)=\frac{1-i}{5}.
\]

Moreover,

\[
\mu(A)=\frac15,\quad
\mu(B)=\frac25,\quad
\mu(A\sqcup B)=1,\quad
I_2(A,B)=1-\frac15-\frac25=\frac25\ne0.
\]

Thus both the off-diagonal decoherence-functional entry and observable
second-order interference are nonzero exactly.

Hermiticity follows by conjugation. Strong positivity is not inferred from a
numeric eigenvalue calculation: for arbitrary event amplitudes \(a_i\),
\(D_{ij}=\bar a_i a_j\) is a rank-one Gram matrix and

\[
\sum_{ij}\bar z_iD_{ij}z_j
=\left|\sum_i a_i z_i\right|^2\ge0.
\]

The grade-2 identity was also evaluated exactly for every disjoint singleton
geometry triple through the declared cutoff.
