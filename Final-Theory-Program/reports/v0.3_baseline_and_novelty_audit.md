## Novelty boundary

The v0.3 open targets are:

1. whether the recorded sparse-Kraus histories carry nonzero, representation-
   invariant geometric interference;
2. whether spectator reduction is definable and satisfied at channel/instrument
   level;
3. mechanically generated CPOBC relations beyond hand-entered fixtures and an
   exact, explicitly scoped \(d\geq3\) representation search;
4. separate infinite-extension results for classical outcomes, a CP instrument,
   and a decoherence functional.

Appendix-wide continuous-parameter reconstruction, TOBC/NTOBC re-search,
large-\(N\) sampling, continuum scaling, and every Spin-2 or Einstein gate are
outside this version.
