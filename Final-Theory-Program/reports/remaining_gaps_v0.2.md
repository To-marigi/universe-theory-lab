1. Complete the continuous-parameter exact reproduction of the QSG appendix.
2. Define and certify the full CPOBC relation set used by a representation
   search.
3. Expand the symbolic ansatz beyond the six-matrix rational library, including
   block, non-normal, singular-declared, and algebraic-number candidates.
4. Prove or falsify spectator independence and quantum Bell causality for the
   sparse Kraus profile.
5. Establish an infinite covariant-event extension criterion for the chosen
   profile.
6. Build a scalable, detailed-balance-checked candidate sampler.
7. Evaluate at separated sizes with independent dimension and correlation
   length estimators.
8. Add coarse-graining and held-out symmetry reconstruction.

candidate-derived two-point functions or attempt spin classification.
