# v0.3 infinite covariant-event extension

The extension question separates three objects. The aggregate status remains
`INFINITE_EXTENSION_BLOCKED` because the full CP instrument lacks a common
infinite-time output space, even though two simpler objects admit proofs.

## A. Classical recorded-outcome measure

Verdict: `CLASSICAL_OUTCOME_EXTENSION_PASS`.

For every finite source causal set:

1. its down-set precursor collection is finite and nonempty;
2. every candidate local weight is a positive rational;
3. the finite sum of aggregate orbit weights is positive;
4. division by that sum defines an exactly normalised stochastic kernel.

Recursive kernel products therefore define a consistent family of
finite-cylinder probabilities. Every stage outcome space is finite discrete,
so the Ionescu–Tulcea/Kolmogorov extension theorem supplies a unique countably
additive probability measure on the infinite product sigma algebra. Restricting
that measure to the label-invariant sub-sigma algebra preserves countable
additivity.

This is an all-stage argument from the kernel definition. It is not inferred
from a flat finite sequence. As a regression, exhaustive path counts through
\(n=5\) are \(1,1,2,6,28,195\); total variation is exactly one at every tested
stage and every child marginal equals its parent distribution.

The proof obligations and exact finite regression are independently rechecked
from `certificates/infinite_extension/classical_diagonal_extension_v0.3.json`.

The scope is the sigma algebra generated by recorded automorphism-orbit branch
cylinders. It is not a statement about arbitrary nonmeasurable subsets,
manifold emergence, or a continuum limit.

## B. Operator-valued CP instrument

Verdict: `INFINITE_EXTENSION_BLOCKED`.

The finite root-space effects scalarise to \(p(h)I\) on a one-dimensional input
and hence extend as a trivial POVM. That is not yet an infinite CP instrument.
The repository has:

- stage-dependent output Hilbert spaces;
- no declared common output operator algebra;
- no isometric inductive embeddings;
- no normal CP reduction maps proving output consistency.

The complex scalar bounded-variation theorem of arXiv:2003.11311v1 does not
create these missing structures. No operator-measure PASS is issued.

## C. Decoherence functional

Verdict: `DECOHERENCE_FUNCTIONAL_EXTENSION_PASS`, with a classical-diagonal
claim boundary.

The v0.2 profile explicitly records and decoheres branch outcomes. Once the
classical probability measure \(\mu\) above exists, define

\[
D(A,B)=\mu(A\cap B).
\]

This construction is Hermitian, normalised and countably biadditive. Strong
positivity follows because for finite event and coefficient families,

\[
\sum_{ij}\bar c_i c_jD(A_i,A_j)
=
\left\|\sum_i c_i\,1_{A_i}\right\|_{L^2(\mu)}^2\geq0.
\]

Thus this particular diagonal functional extends; the conclusion is not
obtained by merely renaming the classical measure. It remains a level-1
classical decoherence functional with no off-diagonal geometric interference.
A future non-diagonal profile would require a new extension proof.
