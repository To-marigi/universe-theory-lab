kinematics, but no microscopic dynamics, continuum phase, emergent
Lorentz/rotation symmetry, excitation spectrum, or spin-classification map.

```text
KINEMATICS_DEFINED

SPIN_CLASSIFICATION_NOT_YET_DEFINED
```
