# v0.3 Geometry Interference Gate

Verdict: `CLASSICAL_GEOMETRY_WITH_QUANTUM_MEMORY`.

This verdict means that the geometry sector is exactly classical in the tested
and declared finite domain. “Quantum memory” refers only to the orthogonal
which-outcome register of the fixed instrument; it is not a noncommuting
internal matter sector and is not evidence for quantum spacetime.

## Semantics before calculation

The v0.2 object is a fixed outcome instrument, not an unspecified channel
unravelling. One physical outcome is stored for each automorphism-orbit growth
move, after the orbit multiplicity is included in its exact probability. A
fine-grained history is therefore a sequence of these fixed outcomes and carries
an orthogonal environment record.

For histories at one fixed cardinality,

\[
D(h,h')
=
\sqrt{p_h p_{h'}}\,
\delta_{C_h,C_{h'}}\,
\delta_{r_h,r_{h'}}.
\]

The record delta is part of the declared instrument semantics. Removing it and
coherently summing different Kraus outcomes produces another instrument, not a
different representation of the same recorded experiment.

The basis does not contain every vertex-labelled history. Three quotient levels
are kept separate at \(n=5\):

- 195 fixed-instrument outcome paths;
- 357 birth-labelled histories collapsed into those paths;
- 63 unlabelled final causal sets.

The natural-label fibres independently match linear-extension and automorphism
counts.

## Kraus-representation boundary

Unitary mixing of Kraus operators may preserve the channel while changing the
fixed outcome instrument and its event projectors. Such mixed indices are not
accepted as the same physical histories. Within a fixed one-Kraus outcome, a
phase or equivalent Kraus representation leaves its CP map and the diagonal
functional unchanged.

The following false inferences are explicitly killed:

- incoherent branch alternatives treated as a coherent Kraus sum;
- every off-diagonal forced to zero without checking record/system overlaps;
- arbitrary rotated Kraus labels promoted to histories;
- labelled paths, orbit paths and unlabelled endpoints conflated.

## Scope

The initial state is the unique empty-causet ray. Arbitrary initial
superpositions, cardinalities above five, environment erasure, a non-diagonal
microscopic profile and infinite-time events are not part of this finite gate.
The separate extension report addresses the diagonal infinite-time object.
