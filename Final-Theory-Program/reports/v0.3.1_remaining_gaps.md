# Final-Theory Bench v0.3.1 — remaining gaps

The main target remains open. The completed calculation searched dimension 3
over `Q(t)` in one gauge-fixed, equal-diagonal upper-triangular
scaled-Heisenberg ansatz. It touches
`D3_JORDAN_3`, the `D3_JORDAN_2_PLUS_1` boundary, and
`D3_REDUCIBLE_INDECOMPOSABLE`; it does not exhaust any of them.

## Representation algebra

- assign exact invertible 3×3 operators to all 165 transition occurrences;
- solve all 641 compiled CPOBC relations together with MSR and GC;
- decompose the unrestricted components of all seven declared d=3 Jordan and
  reducibility strata;
- control residual similarity gauge and deduplicate exact components;
- distinguish reducible, indecomposable, triangularisable, and Burnside-
  irreducible components;
- determine noncommutative ideal dependencies beyond the exact finite
  provenance graph.

The available encoding contains 7,047 d=3 Bell-relation entry equations before
adding all global MSR, GC, and inverse constraints. No dense full-system
Gröbner basis, primary decomposition, modular-prime survey, or unbounded
component search was run. There was one exact ansatz class, one free parameter,
one noncommutativity-saturation branch, and no numerical candidate generation.

## Physical QSG

- construct the paper-defined vector measure only after a full exact
  representation exists;
- certify normalization, Hermiticity, strong positivity, quotient and path
  consistency;
- find physically distinct exclusive covariant geometry events with exact
  nonzero off-diagonal D and I2;
- separate initial-vector dependence from representation-invariant claims.

The scalar ℂSG interference witness is a positive-control regression and does
not close these gaps.

## Infinite extension

- identify a vector pre-measure and applicable theorem;
- establish cylinder consistency and all weak scalarisations;
- prove the required total-variation or semivariation bound.

Finite n evidence cannot close the infinite-extension gap.

## Separate formulation track

Kraus-channel spectator reduction remains a future, separately versioned
problem. Quantum Causal Histories, semicausal channels, combs, process tensors,
and causal boxes do not supply CPOBC without an explicit equivalence theorem.

d=4 was not executed because d=3 has neither a stratum-wide no-go nor a
construction that motivates a specific higher-dimensional ansatz.
