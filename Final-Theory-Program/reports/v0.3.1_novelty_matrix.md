## Novelty rule

v0.3.1 receives scientific novelty credit only for results strictly beyond the
audited primary-source boundary. Reproducing a theorem, definition, example, or
failed ansatz from a paper is a regression result even when the reproduction is
exact or newly automated.

The decisive boundary is [Srivastava and Surya,
arXiv:2603.25503v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25503v1): nonsingular TOBC and
NTOBC commutative collapse, the CPOBC relations explicitly derived there, the
conditional antichain-center collapse, and the tested Pauli inconsistency are
locked. The general higher-dimensional noncommutative CPOBC representation
problem remains open.

## Locked theorem and regression ledger

The following labels must remain visible in implementation outputs:

- `KNOWN_PAPER_RELATION` for a paper equation;
- `GENERATED_EQUIVALENT_TO_PAPER` for a mechanically generated equivalent;
- `GENERATED_DEPENDENT_RELATION` when dependence on locked relations is
  certified;
- `NEW_RELATION_CANDIDATE` when noncommutative ideal membership or another
  dependency question remains unresolved.

Automation does not turn `KNOWN_PAPER_RELATION` into a discovery. Likewise, an
exact reproduction of the scalar CSG control establishes
`COMMUTATIVE_CSG_REFERENCE_PASS`, not a new quantum dynamics.

## Claim ceiling

Even a successful representation and physical finite-stage certificate would
remain a candidate microscopic QSG dynamics. It would not establish continuum
spacetime, Lorentz symmetry, Spin-2, a graviton, Einstein dynamics, or a final
theory. The required overall verdict remains `FINAL_THEORY_OPEN`.
