# Dynamics formalism comparison

| Profile | Mathematical object | Normalization notion | v0.2 role |
|---|---|---|---|
| sparse Kraus growth | rank-one branch instruments | sum of `K†K` at each source | central candidate |
| complex sequential growth | scalar complex vector measure | histories measure plus extension criterion | positive extension control |
| operator QSG | noncommuting transition algebra | operator Markov sum rule and QBC | research track |
| BDG path sum | complete-history action weight | regulated partition/path sum | GR-like positive control |
| random growth | equal local down-set weight | exact branch sum | negative control |

These objects are not coerced into one interface beyond shared metadata.
In particular, Kraus completeness does not substitute for a decoherence-
functional extension theorem, and scalar complex growth does not certify an
operator-valued QSG algebra.
