# Held-out protocol v0.2

Reserved partitions are:

- causal-set size `n=5`;
- sign-reversed link-fugacity region;
- two-element-chain initial history;
- timid growth branch;
- QSG representation dimension four;
- finite chain control family;
- finite antichain non-manifoldlike family.

Fugacities, normalization, operator ordering, exact-equality policy, and the
absence of a classifier threshold were frozen before held-out evaluation.
Nothing was retuned.

The held-out result supports only the conservative negative/bounded conclusion.
It does not make `n=5` a continuum test.
