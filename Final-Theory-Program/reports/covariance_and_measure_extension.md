- `BACKGROUND_METRIC_INDEPENDENCE`: no continuum metric is a candidate input.
- `DIMENSION_INDEPENDENCE`: no target dimension is a candidate input.
- `PHYSICAL_BACKGROUND_INDEPENDENCE`: not yet established for continuum

fifth cannot be tested before physical continuum observables exist.

a separate infinite-event problem; it is not inferred from finite
normalization.
