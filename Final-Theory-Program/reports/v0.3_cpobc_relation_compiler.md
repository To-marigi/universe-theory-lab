# v0.3 CPOBC relation compiler

Verdict: `CPOBC_RELATION_COMPILER_PARTIAL`.

The finite combinatorics is complete in its declared domain, but the operator
compiler covers only the Eq. 103–106 core. This distinction prevents a finite
transition-graph enumeration from being reported as the full CPOBC algebra.

## Generated domain

For every unlabelled source causal set at stages one through four, the compiler:

1. enumerates all down-set transitions;
2. quotients transitions and ordered precursor pairs by the source
   automorphism group;
3. deletes common spectators and canonically hashes the decorated core;
4. groups pair instances into Bell families;
5. instantiates cross-stage Eq. 103–106 words with exact transition IDs.

The output contains:

- 131 transition orbits;
- 373 precursor-pair orbits;
- 146 Bell families;
- 641 cross-stage relations;
- 71 equal-size and 570 unequal-size relations.

Every relation records source and target IDs/hashes, stages, transition pairs,
precursors, spectators, cardinalities, operator order, invertibility
assumptions, automorphism multiplicities, GC and MSR boundaries,
denominator-cleared noncommutative words, paper correspondence, dependency and
novelty classification.

The outgoing down-set orbit partitions match their exact raw multiplicities.
All relation and pair hashes are unique. Equal-size instances contain both
orientations and Eq. 104; denominator-cleared forms do not require inverses.

## Partial boundary

GC is used only for canonical endpoint bookkeeping and MSR only for exact
outgoing partitions. Operator-valued GC path identities and MSR eliminations,
paper Eqs. 107–163, and Appendix dependencies are not compiled. No
noncommutative ideal membership or independent second derivation was run.
Therefore:

- zero new independent relations are claimed;
- every generated record is a finite instance of known paper relations;
- compiler success is not evidence for a representation.
