# Final-Theory Bench v0.3 remaining gaps

The critical path stops before a quantum-spacetime candidate. The gaps are
ordered by scientific dependency.

## 1. Non-diagonal geometry dynamics

The current physical outcome record diagonalises every geometry history. A new
profile must specify a canonical instrument/process semantics in which
coarse-grained covariant geometry events have nonzero interference without
depending on an arbitrary Kraus decomposition. It must retain exact strong
positivity, normalisation and label-quotient consistency.

## 2. A typed spectator reduction

Kraus Bell causality needs spectator-labelled local algebras, subsystem
embeddings or another canonical reduction construction. The full and reduced
outcome algebras must be related by CPTP/TNI maps before any Choi or
superoperator equality can be called a physical Bell law.

## 3. Complete Track-A relation compilation

The compiler must add operator-valued GC paths, MSR eliminations, paper
Eqs. 107–163 and Appendix dependencies. Any proposed new relation then needs
noncommutative ideal membership or an equivalent dependency test and an
independent second derivation.

## 4. Representation theorem or exact construction

The 14 exact point checks should be replaced by parameter-complete strata:
dense symbolic, weighted shift, permutation-plus-diagonal, complete block and
reducible-but-indecomposable cases, with a simultaneous-similarity quotient.
A FOUND result must satisfy the full compiled relation assignment. A no-go must
state assumptions and carry elimination or structural proof certificates.

## 5. Infinite CP instrument

The finite output rays need a common operator algebra, compatible embeddings
and normal CP reduction maps. Only then can an operator-valued/vector-measure
extension theorem be applied. The scalar complex bounded-variation result is
not a replacement.

## 6. Initial-state and environment semantics

The present root state is one-dimensional. Future work must define nontrivial
initial density operators and decide whether the outcome environment is
physical, accessible, erased or coarse-grained.

## Deferred to v0.4 or later

Only after the geometry and Bell gates are repaired should the project add a
large-\(N\) forward sampler, auxiliary fixed-\(N\) MCMC, finite-size scaling,
dimension/correlation estimators and coarse-graining. Two-point functions,
tensor/Spin classification, gravitons and Einstein recovery remain later gates,
not v0.3 work.
