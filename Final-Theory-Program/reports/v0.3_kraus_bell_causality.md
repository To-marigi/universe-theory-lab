# v0.3 Kraus channel Bell causality

Verdict: `KRAUS_BELL_CAUSALITY_UNDEFINED`.

This is not a failed CPOBC test. The inverse-based operator relations of Track A
were never applied to the singular rank-one matrix units of Track B.

## Definition audit

The present Hilbert schema assigns one ray to each unlabelled causal history.
It does not assign a spectator subsystem, a tensor product decomposition, a
canonical partial trace, or a full-to-reduced CP channel. Therefore the proposed
equation

\[
R_{\rm target}\circ\mathcal E_{\rm full}
=
\mathcal E_{\rm reduced}\circ R_{\rm source}
\]

cannot yet be typed as an equality of maps in this model. Declaring the
one-dimensional spaces abstractly isomorphic would erase the geometry that the
reduction is supposed to test and would not define an outcome-algebra
correspondence.

Combinatorial spectator deletion is still useful as a diagnostic: for a pair
of labelled precursors, remove vertices in neither precursor and take the
induced subposet. On the unlabelled orbit quotient this construction is not
always unique. At source cardinality at most five, 88 aggregate branch pairs
have multiple reduced-family alignments and 100 configurations collapse two
different full outcomes to one reduced outcome.

## Exact finite diagnostics

All sources through \(n=5\) and their one-step transitions were inspected:

- 88 unlabelled source histories;
- 708 exact instrument outcomes;
- 865 canonical reduced Bell-family signatures;
- 2,151 configurations with at least one common spectator;
- 1,944 unequal-precursor and 207 equal-precursor spectator cases.

For every source, CP, trace preservation of the outcome instrument and history
channel, trace non-increase of each outcome, and positive-semidefinite diagonal
Choi data pass exactly. All calculations use integers and rational numbers.
Adjacent-transposition tests cover every source and 3,532 precursor images;
relabel covariance passes.

The local per-labelled-precursor weights satisfy the spectator product
diagnostic with zero violations. Absolute normalised probabilities may change
because the full and reduced sources have different outgoing normalisations.
After precursor automorphism orbits are aggregated, the naive probability-
product relation fails in 798 configurations. These are exact counterexamples
to that naive orbit-level proposal, not certificates that a correctly typed
channel Bell law fails.

## Representation-invariant comparison

Channel equality is compared through exact superoperators and Choi matrices.
An exact \(3\)-\(4\)-\(5\) orthogonal mixing of two dephasing-channel Kraus
operators changes the Kraus list while preserving the Choi matrix,
superoperator and trace-preservation effect. A Kraus-string comparison would
therefore report a false difference and is rejected.

Omitting orbit multiplicities also changes an exact instrument probability
vector, Choi matrix and superoperator; the first finite witness kills this
mutation.

## Missing structure required for a future PASS or FAIL

A future profile must supply:

1. spectator-labelled subsystem embeddings or an equivalent local operator
   algebra;
2. canonical full/reduced outcome correspondence;
3. CPTP/TNI reduction maps that respect the unlabelled quotient;
4. a Choi, superoperator, instrument or process-tensor equality on every Bell
   family.

Until those maps exist, neither `KRAUS_BELL_CAUSALITY_PASS` nor
`KRAUS_BELL_CAUSALITY_FAIL` is scientifically licensed.
